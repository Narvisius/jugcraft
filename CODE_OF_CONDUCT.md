# Community conduct

Treat contributors and players respectfully. Give specific feedback about ideas and code. No harassment, threats, hate, doxxing, credential sharing, or malicious submissions. Do not pressure volunteers to work or merge changes.

Credit contributions. Disclose uncertainty and test limitations. Do not fabricate AI attribution or evidence. Maintainers may moderate content, close submissions, or restrict participation to protect the community, with an explanation where appropriate.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a contact they publish. Do not post private personal information in public issues. Maintainers must publish a contact route before a wider community launch.
