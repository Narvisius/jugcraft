# The Arcane Concordance: a player's guide

The Arcane Concordance is Jugcraft's magic: research you earn by looking at the world, spells cast from your own
**Focus**, workshops, gardens, circles, workers and the sky, rising through five stages to a **Concord Spire** that
changes the land round it. This guide is for players. The in-game codex (*Arcane Concordance*) and your **Concordance
Journal** (J) say the same things in the game. Designers and contributors want [ARCANE_CONCORDANCE.md](ARCANE_CONCORDANCE.md).

Nobody has yet played the whole of the Concordance from start to finish; see the
[journey record](features/arcane-concordance-journey.md). If something here does not match the game, the game is right
and this guide is a bug.

## Your first spell

1. **Find something that glows**: amethyst shards (in geodes), glow lichen or glow berries (in lush caves), a glow ink
   sac (from glow squid), glowstone dust, or Jugcraft's glowcap, glimmerbloom and jack-o'-lantern mushroom.
2. **Sneak and use it** to examine it. You are now an **Initiate** of First Light.
3. **In the dark** (a cave, or outside at night), examine three *different* ones. Or build a **Lampwright's Bench** and
   study one there. You now **understand** First Light and know three invocations: Aegis, Kindle and Revelation.
4. **Craft an Initiate's Wand**: something luminous, a stick and a copper ingot. Hold it in your main hand.
5. **Cast.** The use key casts the first invocation on the spell bar, and 2, 3 and on cast the ones after it. While the
   wand is in your hand those number keys cast instead of changing your hotbar slot; scroll to change slot.

| Do | How |
|---|---|
| Examine a specimen | Sneak and use it |

| Open your journal | J (remappable in Controls) |
| Concordance settings | Its key in Controls (unbound at first), or Mod Menu if installed |
| Look at a device's state | Jade, if installed; otherwise the device's own messages and the journal |

## Focus, research and stages

- **Focus** is your own attention: 20 points, one back every two seconds. Every invocation and composed spell spends
  it. Nothing stores or trades it.
- **Research** has four states: **encountered**, **observed**, **understood**, **mastered**. You can also understand an
  entry by reading another player's **Research Notes**. Notes pass on instructions, never experience, so you still
  observe and master it yourself.
- Your **stage** rises as your research does, and is never taken away.

## Three ways to a Spire

You may take research in any order. Three routes show how different the climb can be:

- **Cultivation.** Verdant Beds, crops that grow only where their habitat suits them, brews from the harvest, and
  helpers bound to the garden. It ends in a **Verdant Spire** that grows the crops round it.
- **Exploration and combat.** The sky from an Orrery Observatory, a Thornheart Blade grown by deeds, relics on the
  road, and curses laid by touch. It goes to the Nether, and ends in a **Star Spire** that restores its keepers' Focus
  and reveals hostile creatures.
- **Crafting and infrastructure.** Circles powered by Ley Pylons, rings forged at the Artificer's Bench, matter weighed
  at the Assayer's Scale, and porters fetching to Courier Posts. It ends in a **Lantern Spire** that lights the dark so
  nothing hostile spawns there.

Each route, step by step: [the journey record](features/arcane-concordance-journey.md).

## Playing with others

- A **party** shares Lanternward's protection, Conclave lecterns and projects, and the keeping of a spire. Your
  research, Focus, rings and bound relics stay your own.
- Rituals for two need two players, and a participant who leaves stops them.
- Your spells, devices and workers change only what you could change by hand: claims, towns, spawn protection and the
  PvP rules apply to them. While you are away (offline or in another dimension) they wait, unless the server lets them
  carry on in your name.

## When something goes wrong

- **Read the signs.** Grey smoke and sinking ash with a hollow falling tone mean a **shortage**: the dry pylon, a
  missing ingredient, a missed upkeep. Flame and sparks with a sharp rising tone mean **danger**: failing containment,
  a searing stir, a damaged spire. Both have subtitles.
- **Ask your journal** (J): it says what each research state and each stage route still needs, in words.
- **Nothing is lost by accident.** A broken circle releases its offerings; a broken anchor, crucible, post or Spire
  Heart drops what it held; a cancelled request gives its items back; dying in a dream returns what you carried, once.

## Your world

The Concordance saves with a version, so later Jugcraft versions can read and update your world. **Back up your world
before moving to an older Jugcraft**: it cannot read the newer saves.
