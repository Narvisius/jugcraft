# The Arcane Concordance

Jugcraft's magic: a practice learned by observing the world, studied at workstations and cast through instruments. It
is not a mana bar. This document is the contract every Concordance feature builds on: who owns which state, how
content loads, the vocabulary, which library does what, and where each of the 32 roadmap steps stands.

Status: the 32 roadmap steps are on `main` (merged through #263 on 8 October 2026). Section 7 gives each step's status
and record. Step 31's player journey is checked by machine, but no person has played it yet. After step 32, Ember's
first part (Hearthbinding and four fire invocations) and second part (the Hearthbinder's regalia, made from the owner's
fire art) are on `main` too (integrated by #277): see their records, [part 1](features/arcane-concordance-ember.md) and
[part 2](features/arcane-concordance-ember-regalia.md). So are the first trinkets part, the owner's belt, boots and charms
worn once Relic Lore is understood ([Wayfaring](features/arcane-concordance-trinkets.md)), and part 1b, drawing the belt
and boot on the body; part 1c (the belt and boots hidden under armour, and each player's Show my worn trinkets choice,
seen by everyone) is on `claude/awesome-davinci-iwv3b9`. Where to start:
- the [delivery record](features/arcane-concordance-delivery.md) indexes the evidence, the library integration matrix
  and what remains;
- players start with [the guide](ARCANE_CONCORDANCE_GUIDE.md).

The names below (Principles, resources, stages, traditions) were new proposals at step 2: no earlier Jugcraft document
defined a magic taxonomy.

## 1. Architecture contract (step 1)

**Platform.** Minecraft Java 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Java 25 ([PLATFORM.md](PLATFORM.md)); libraries pinned in
`distribution/frameworks.lock.json` ([FRAMEWORKS.md](FRAMEWORKS.md)). Nothing here changes a pin or adds a dependency.

**Authoritative state.** The logical server owns every Concordance fact. Clients receive copies for display and never
send one back; a client sends only intent (use an item, press a menu button, request a Spell Engine cast), and the
server re-derives everything from its own state.

| State | Owner and storage | Saved | Sent to clients |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rules: research, invocations, workings | Data packs, `data/<ns>/concordance/{research,invocation,working}/*.json`, loaded by `ConcordanceData` (a reload listener) into an immutable `ConcordanceRules` | No (data) | No; clients need only what the player knows |
| A player's knowledge (research states, evidence keys, learned invocations) | `jugcraft:concordance_knowledge` player attachment (`Knowledge`) | Yes, with the player; kept through death | To that player only |
| A player's Focus | `jugcraft:concordance_focus` player attachment (`FocusPool`: stored amount and a game-time stamp) | Yes, kept through death | To that player only |
| A lantern's Radiance | `jugcraft:radiance` item component (`LanternCharge`: stored and a game-time stamp) and `jugcraft:lantern_lit` | Yes, with the item | Yes (item sync) |
| A bench's study and pending notes | `LampwrightBenchBlockEntity` (owner UUID, item id, progress) | Yes, with the chunk | Menu data for the viewer; a Jade snapshot without names |
| A Kindled light's remaining life | `lumen_mote` block state (`age`, `trail`) and a scheduled block tick | Yes, with the chunk | Block state |
| Research Notes | `jugcraft:research_notes` item component (author, entries up to understood) | Yes, with the item | Yes (item sync) |
| A Lumen Sconce's Radiance and owner | `LumenSconceBlockEntity` (a `Reservoir`, a burn clock, the placer) | Yes, with the chunk; kept on the item when broken | Block state (`lit`); a Jade snapshot of the charge |
| Spell cooldowns | Spell Engine's cooldown manager | Spell Engine | Spell Engine |

**Content loading.** Jugcraft's own rules (research, invocations, workings and conversions) are generated by
`tools/concordance.py` and checked by `tools/check_mod_data.py`. At load and `/reload`, `RulesParser` rejects malformed files with a named reason
(unknown fields, bad ids, out-of-order states, newer schemas, channels without loss), and `ConcordanceRules.build`
drops entries with missing prerequisites, cycles or orphan unlocks and reports them. `/jugcraft concordance diagnose`
lists the reasons. Player state is never deleted because a rule disappeared: entries for unknown research are kept as
they are, so removing and restoring a data pack loses nothing.

**Pure core.** `concordance/rules/` holds the research engine, the parser, Focus and the knowledge model, and
`concordance/resource/` the typed resource rules (containers, transfers, conversions, allocation, ledgers), with no
Minecraft types, so they are deterministic and testable outside the game.

**Budgets.** Nothing the Concordance adds runs per player per tick. Focus regenerates lazily from game time, lantern
burn is lazy from game time, a Kindled light uses one scheduled tick per 80 ticks (a lantern's trail light one per 10),
a lit lantern in hand places at most one light per 10 ticks, examination is limited to one per player per 10 ticks,
and bench status is worked out at most once per tick per open screen. Evidence is capped at 128 keys per research
entry.

**Scope.** Single player and cooperative multiplayer, server-authoritative. No combat magic yet; lighting is the only
world change and obeys the same protections as placing a block (world bounds, spawn protection, `mayUseItemAt`, town
protection). Expected scale: a few research entries per milestone, tens of players per server.

## 2. Vocabulary (step 2, proposed)

### Ten Principles

A Principle is knowledge and affinity, never a currency. Where combat scaling fits, it maps to an existing Spell Power
school (no new school is registered).

| Principle | Domain | Spell Power school |
|---|---|---|
| Radiance | light, revelation and sight | `spell_power:arcane` |
| Ember | heat, combustion and change by fire | `spell_power:fire` |
| Rime | cold, stillness and preservation | `spell_power:frost` |
| Tempest | storm, charge and sudden motion | `spell_power:lightning` |
| Strata | stone, structure and weight | none |
| Verdance | growth, life and renewal | `spell_power:healing` |
| Tide | flow, solution and exchange | none |
| Tether | binding, sympathy and agreements | none |
| Echo | memory, sound, dreams and knowledge | `spell_power:arcane` |
| Hollow | absence, decay and endings | `spell_power:soul` |

### Seven resources

None converts into another except through an explicit conversion recipe, and every conversion loses. The rules
that enforce this are in `concordance/resource/` (step 7; see the sharing record).

| Resource | Kind | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | personal attention | point | A practitioner's own capacity to cast. Returns with time, lives with the player; never stored in an item, traded or carried by a device. **Implemented** (20 points, one back every 40 ticks). |
| Ley Charge | operational energy | ley | Stored working energy in devices and conduits. Powers stations and rituals; not light, heat or life. |
| Principle Essence | material value | measure | A measured quantity of one Principle drawn from a specimen. Essences of different Principles never substitute. Milestone 1's Radiance in a lantern is the first essence. |
| Vitae | biological expenditure | drop | Life given deliberately in an offering; separate from health and from the exhaustion it leaves. |
| Astral Resonance | celestial alignment | resonance | Alignment gathered when the sky is right; recorded per event so one alignment never pays twice. |
| Bound Will | supernatural identity | record | An agreement with a named spirit or construct: an obligation with an identity, never an anonymous fluid. |
| Prima Materia | equivalence value | grain | The exact value assigned to an eligible mundane material for transmutation, rounded down, never created from nothing. |

### Five stages

Initiate (Jugcraft's Discovery stage) > Practitioner (Workshops) > Adept (Specialization) > Master (Expeditions) >
Architect (Shared wonders). See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

### Research states

Each research entry, for each player: **Encountered** > **Observed** > **Understood** > **Mastered**. Each state lists
one or more alternative evidence rules; one piece of evidence may carry an entry through several states.

### Glossary

- **Specimen**: an item the Concordance can learn from (`#jugcraft:luminous_specimens` for Radiance).
- **Evidence**: something the server saw a player do that research can learn from: an examination (item and light
  level), a finished study (item and station) or an invocation that took effect (invocation and chunk). Each distinct
  piece counts once.
- **Invocation**: an authored spell a player may cast once their research reaches a state; cast through Spell Engine.
- **Instrument**: an item that casts Concordance invocations from the main hand (`#jugcraft:concordance_instruments`).
- **Working**: a process at a station (craft, infuse, channel) that a player's research must allow.
- **Radiance (charge)**: the Radiance essence (`essence/radiance`) held in a Kindled Lantern or Lumen Sconce, in
  measures.
- **Research Notes**: a shared record of what a player had begun of each entry, up to understood. Reading another
  player's notes is evidence where an entry accepts it; it is never the reader's own observation or practice.
- **Personal knowledge, shared records, practical competency**: what a player has observed and understood
  themselves; what they have read in someone else's notes; and mastery, earned only by their own practice.
- **Container, transfer, conversion**: a container holds one resource type with a capacity and a stated overflow; a
  transfer moves one type between containers under ownership and rate rules and never converts; a conversion is an
  explicit, data-defined recipe that always loses.
- **Composition, component, instrument capacity**: a spell written as words in order (delivery, selection,
  operations, optional ending; modifiers joined with `+`; `then` for a branch). Each word is a data-defined component
  learnt from research and taking some of the instrument's capacity.
- **Plan, ledger, work**: what a composition compiles to (a bounded tree with its Focus, cooldown and limits); the
  per-event account every application is admitted to (no repeats, no more distinct targets or work than compiled);
  work is the unit applications are counted in.
- **Effect, cause, stacking, tolerance**: one of ten common operations with its numbers; who and what it came from
  (kept through triggered effects); how a lasting effect combines with one already there; how a creature bears harmful
  control (normal, resistant, immune).
- **Attunement, resonance, instability, Discord, identity, mastery**: reserved for later steps with the meanings in the
  resource table; *mastery* is the Mastered research state, earned by practice (milestone 1: Kindle cast in 8 chunks).

## 4. One authority per cast (step 4 policy for spells)

For every Concordance invocation:

- **Charging path:** Jugcraft's Focus only. The spell's own costs are zero (`exhaust` 0, `durability` 0, no item).
- **Gate:** `ConcordanceSpells.attempt` on Spell Engine's `CASTING_ATTEMPT.PRE`, on both sides: feature on, an
  instrument in the main hand, the invocation learned, enough Focus. It only ever refuses; it never returns success, so
  Spell Engine's own cooldown check still runs.
- **Impact commitment:** the `CUSTOM` impact (server only) re-checks the gate and does the world change through the
  shared effect boundary (`ConcordanceEffects`). It reports failure if nothing changed.
- **Cooldown:** Spell Engine's, from the spell's `cost.cooldown`, applied only on a successful delivery.
- **Payment:** Focus is taken once, in `COST_CONSUME`, which Spell Engine calls only after a successful delivery.
- **Refund policy:** none is needed. A refused or failed cast takes no Focus and starts no cooldown.
- **Presentation:** animations, sounds and particles never decide an outcome.

Inscribed spells (step 8) follow the same policy through the carrier spell `jugcraft:composed`: the gate checks the
instrument and the inscription's stated Focus (all the client can know); the `CUSTOM` impact compiles the inscription
again on the server and runs the plan under its ledger, failing if nothing took effect; `COST_CONSUME` takes the plan's
Focus once and sets the plan's own cooldown. Once a composed spell has taken effect its later pulses are not refunded.
Every Concordance effect, invoked or composed, is applied by `ConcordanceEffects` (step 9).

## 5. Persistent definitions (step 3)

| Definition | Shared (data) | Per instance | Schema |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research entry | `concordance/research/*.json`: principle, tradition, stage, icon, requires, states with evidence rules, unlocks | `Knowledge.Progress` per player | `schema: 1` |
| Invocation | `concordance/invocation/*.json`: spell, principle, research, state, Focus costs | learned cost in `Knowledge.invocations` | `schema: 1` |
| Working | `concordance/working/*.json`: type, station, research, state, work item, specimens, result, Radiance, Focus | none | `schema: 1` |
| Spell | `spell/*.json` (Spell Engine format) | Spell Engine cooldown | Spell Engine |
| Lantern charge | item definition: capacity 64, burn 400 ticks | `jugcraft:radiance` component | record fields |
| Conversion | `concordance/conversion/*.json`: from and to resource types and amounts | none | `schema: 1` |
| Component | `concordance/component/*.json`: slot, research state, capacity, Focus, and its delivery, selection, operation, modifier or ending | none | `schema: 1` |
| Instrument | `concordance/instrument/*.json`: item, capacity, targets, work, branches, duration | `jugcraft:inscription` component (text, Focus, cooldown) | `schema: 1` |

Later definitions (ingredient properties, rituals, structures, enhancements, spirit agreements,
construct tasks, organisms, celestial patterns, transmutation eligibility) follow the same pattern when their step
starts: stable id, `schema`, display, tags, dependencies, and a parser that names what is wrong.
