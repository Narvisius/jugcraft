Target milestone and tier: interface only, for every tier's gear. First used by the arms ([arms.md](arms.md) to [arms-vii.md](arms-vii.md)); thallite's gear ([thallite design](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/blob/claude/thallite/docs/features/thallite.md)) is the next to use it.

## Player experience
Gear with traits shows a short tooltip by default, with each trait's **name** and one dim line: **"Hold Shift for details"**. While Shift is held, the tooltip expands: under each name, a brief description of what the trait does, indented and wrapped to a narrow column.

A runebound nodachi, folded:

```
Runebound Nodachi
Long Reach                 (yellow: the kind's trait)
Two-Handed                 (grey)
Weapon Art: Iaido          (gold)
Mark                       (aqua: the boon)
Runebound                  (purple: the line)
Hold Shift for details
```

With Shift held:

```
Runebound Nodachi
Long Reach
  A great curved sword, drawn and swung
  with both hands at a long reach.
Two-Handed
  The blow lands as the swing comes
  round, on every foe in its arc.
Weapon Art: Iaido
  Use: dash ahead; every foe you pass is
  cut a moment later, as the blade comes
  round. Ready again after 8 s.
Mark
  A hit makes the foe glow, seen through
  walls (4 s).
Runebound
  Its runes glow, and mark what it
  strikes.
```

**What uses it in this PR:** every arm (the 34 kinds in both metals and the 32 Arms VII variants), the longbows, arbalests and shields:
- **The kind's trait,** named for what sets the kind apart: Backstab, Parry, Execute, Guard Break, Long Reach and so on (`tools/arms.py` `TRAIT_NAMES`). Its description is the kind's sentence, as before.
- **Two-Handed** on the two-handed kinds.
- **Weapon Art: <name>** on the Arms V kinds, with how to use it and its cooldown.
- **The boon** (Frost, Ember, Drain and the rest) and **the line** (Gilded, Ironclad, Bonecarved, Runebound) of an Arms VII variant.
- **A boss trophy** names its boss ("Trophy of the Crypt Lich"), with no description.
- **Bows and crossbows** add their numbers (draw time, arrow speed, bolt damage) to the description.

**What changes for players today:** the arms' tooltips were the full sentences, always shown, so a variant's tooltip ran to five long lines. They are now five short names and the Shift line, and the sentences come back with Shift. No sentence was lost:
- the kinds' sentences are unchanged, except that three ("Two-handed: ..." and "Two-handed. ...") drop the words "Two-handed", since the Two-Handed trait now says it;
- the arts, boons and lines lost the name at their start ("Drain: each hit heals you half a heart." became the name "Drain" and the description "Each hit heals you half a heart.").

## How it works
- **`gear/TraitTooltips`** (shared code): an item calls it from `appendHoverText`, once per trait with the name's translation key, its colour and its descriptions, then `end()`.
  - Folded, it adds the names and, if any trait has a description, the "Hold Shift for details" line.
  - Expanded, it adds each name with its descriptions under it, wrapped at spaces to 38 characters and indented by two spaces, in grey. A word longer than the column is cut.
- **Reading Shift:** the shared class cannot read the keyboard, so it asks `TraitTooltips.details`. The client sets that to `Minecraft.hasShiftDown()` at start-up (`JugcraftClient`); anywhere else it is off.
- **Live:** a screen rebuilds a hovered item's tooltip every frame, so the tooltip opens and closes as Shift is pressed and released.
- **Language:** a description is wrapped after it is translated, so a translation wraps to its own words.
- **Data:** each trait is two lang lines, the description (`tooltip.jugcraft.arms.<x>`) and its name (`tooltip.jugcraft.arms.<x>.trait`), plus `tooltip.jugcraft.hold_shift`. All are generated by `tools/arms.py` and `tools/arms_variants.py`.
- **Checks:** `tools/check_mod_data.py` fails if any kind, bow, crossbow, shield, weapon art, boon or line lacks its name or description, or if `TRAIT_NAMES` misses a kind.

## Multiplayer and persistence
- **Client only in effect:** tooltips are built on the client. The server never reads the Shift state; `TraitTooltips.details` stays off there.
- **Nothing is saved or sent:** no packets, components or saved data.
- **No IDs change.** The lang keys of the arts, boons and lines keep their names, with new texts; the names are new keys.

## Rollout and open questions
- **Other gear with traits** keeps its plain tooltip lines for now: the exosuit and powered tools' upgrade lines, the scuba gear and the grapple. Each could move to trait names in its own pass.
- **Arms VIII's thrown arms** ([#194](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/pull/194)) need a `TRAIT_NAMES` entry each. Whichever of the two PRs merges second adds them, and `check_mod_data` names any that are missing.
- **The trait names** are first drafts for the owner to rename; each is one line in `tools/arms.py` `TRAIT_NAMES`.
- **Column width:** 38 characters, close to vanilla's widest tooltips. A pixel-exact wrap would need the client's font; the character count is enough for English.
- **Reversible:** reverting restores the always-shown sentences; nothing is saved.
