## Design published before implementation

Checked all 42 open upstream PRs and all public branches of upstream (65) and its four forks (45, 12, 8 and 2). No competing hawthorn or persimmon implementation was identified; the tree-roster branch supplies the existing design. This check cannot cover private or unpushed work.

Hawthorn uses the existing fruitwood, its own dense seasonal leaves and one renewable sapling. Its small wind-bent tree uses a native bending trunk (3 + 0–1, bend length 1) and flat blob crown (radius 2, height 2); the hedge form uses one hidden fruitwood log and native bush foliage (radius 2, offset 1, height 2). Saplings choose the bush three times as often as the tree; sheared leaves also allow deliberate hedge layouts. Following owner inspection, ripe red haws now use the shared right-click fruit harvest: one or two haws, resetting only the fruit stage. Natural supported leaves ripen along exposed sides as well as undersides so low hedges can bear fruit without changing their shapes. Persistent player-placed leaves cannot regrow fruit, and shears/Silk Touch recover foliage without fruit. Haws feed foxes, compost and Cooking Pot Haw Jelly (six haws, two sugar, one Mason Jar, 300 ticks).

Persimmon is an upright tree: native straight trunk 5 + 0–3, oval native fancy foliage (radius 2, offset 1, height 4). The initial blob settings produced a stepped pyramid on the shortest trees in the rendered gallery. Fancy foliage keeps a narrower rounded middle and tapered ends, with a visible lower trunk; this visual correction replaces the planned blob without adding a custom placer. It supplies a full nine-block warm-charcoal wood set, stripping, hand crafting, sawmill and tree-farm routes. It reuses batch 5's seasonal fruit leaves, retaining fruit through autumn and bare winter; exposed supported natural leaves ripen in all calendar modes, including seasons-off, and right-click harvesting yields one or two persimmons and resets only fruit. The fruit is food, compostable and fermentable, feeds a Mason Jar preserve and a Hearth Oven pie. Append its filling to the existing enum so all 26 saved pie/cake filling ordinals remain unchanged.

## Acquisition and existing systems

Collect renewable saplings from ordinary leaves and replant on normal sapling soil. Obstructed growth and unsuitable soil do not destroy the sapling or the obstructing block. Hawthorn's two forms share fruitwood; no duplicate hawthorn timber set is registered. Persimmon's nine-block wood set has axe stripping on every axis, crafting, fuel/tags, sawmill recipes and a renewable tree-farm route. Both saplings use the existing tree farm (six logs and the same sapling, 400 ticks).

Haws are a small snack (1 hunger, 0.1 saturation modifier), fox food and medium compost. Persimmon is food (3 hunger, 0.3 modifier), medium compost and fermentable. Each preserve uses six fruit, two sugar and a Mason Jar in the heated Cooking Pot for 300 ticks; jars retain the current servings/container behavior. Pastry Dough, two persimmons and sugar make a raw persimmon pie, baked in the existing Hearth Oven; slices give 4 hunger and a 0.6 modifier. Existing agriculture consumers and machine routes retain their feature switches; new wood crafting follows `biomes`. Disabling biome generation preserves saved blocks/items and does not rewrite existing chunks.

Fruit ripening retains the shared natural/support rules and never uses bone meal. Hawthorn additionally permits air along an exposed horizontal side; other fruit trees retain the air-below rule. Player-placed leaves cannot ripen, and shears and Silk Touch recover foliage without fruit. Seasonal catch-up remains bounded to 128 leaves and preserves both trees' fruit stages. No new block entity, renderer, dependency, progression gate or world scan was added. The supplied owner library remains untouched; all 162 prior generated wood/foliage images are pixel-identical to the public design commit.

## Hawthorn harvest correction, 16 October 2026

The owner reported that visibly ripe hawthorn leaves did not drop haws when right-clicked and requested a leaf-only fix without repeating the full suite. The original hawthorn block was seasonal foliage with painted fruit and chance-based break drops, so it had no right-click interaction. Hawthorn now uses the existing seasonal fruit block and three real fruit stages in all three foliage looks. Picking ripe leaves produces one or two haws and removes the visible berries; repeat clicks and breaking picked/unripe leaves do not yield more fruit. Natural leaves advance one stage per ten random ticks on average, including seasons-off. Grounded hedge leaves can ripen along exposed sides, and harvested haws drop into adjacent air instead of inside the soil. Other species retain their existing fruit-space and drop rules. Tree shapes, biome placements and recipes are unchanged.

Only **three hawthorn server tests** and **two focused data/art tests** were run for this correction; all passed. The server checks exercise actual empty-hand right-clicks, item drops, duplicate-click prevention, regrowth in every calendar mode, both generated forms, placed/enclosed leaves, decay, ripe/unripe loot and shears/Silk Touch. The data checks cover the ripe-only loot pool and all nine hawthorn model/texture combinations. The preview JAR was rebuilt. The full suite, full data audit and full rendered gallery were not repeated.

Earlier development saves without a hawthorn fruit property load with the normal empty stage and can ripen with random ticks. For the frozen inspection world only, a separate copy retains the old visible fruit: green haws become unripe stage 1 and red haws on gold/bare leaves become ripe stage 2. This prepared copy updates 789 hawthorn palette entries across 175 chunks; every unrelated saved NBT value and the original user save are preserved. The repeatable client gallery now includes all 18 foliage/fruit states on future runs.
