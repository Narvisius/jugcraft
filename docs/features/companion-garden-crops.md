# Additional companion garden crops

Owner-requested implementation on 11 October 2026 using OpenAI Codex (GPT-6). Extends the existing Farmland zones, shared job scheduler, physical cargo, planting selection and linked Supply/Output. Maintenance (fertilizing, tilling, irrigation and tree planting) remains excluded. No new assets, dependencies, item IDs, packets, recipe registrations, fake players or chunk loaders.

## Player behavior and progression

Sugar cane and cactus columns are picked at three blocks, bamboo at four, always retaining the base; columns over sixteen blocks are skipped. Cactus flowers are collected with native drops, including on shorter flowering cacti, to permit further natural growth. Bamboo's retained base is reset to a growing stage. Fully mature sweet berry bushes and berry-bearing cave vines use native interaction loot and retain their plants. Cocoa uses native block loot and one real bean to replant the same face; absent harvest/cargo beans leave the remembered position empty for Supply. Cranberries use their existing pick yield and flowering reset. Flooded rice uses native tall-crop yields/regrowth, real Rice planting items and leaves water when retired due to crop selection changes.

Automatic special planting uses remembered real plants (up to 256 positions per zone). Selecting a compatible ground crop explicitly also permits new planting on valid empty ground within the corners' ground-height range. Cocoa and glow-berry locations always come from player-established plants; cocoa facing is saved. Rice accepts existing shallow source-water plots over its normal bog-soil tag. Normal crop restrictions and source water remain required. No support blocks, water or soil are created. The existing crop selector accepts the newly supported planting items, and Rice Panicles normalize to Rice.

Ground corners still span at most 16 by 16 blocks with three blocks of ground-height difference. Farmland discovery now includes two extra cell layers so pods and vines up to three blocks above the higher ground corner can be recognized. Ordinary farmland and new ground planting remain limited to the original ground-height range. Cocoa/vines use the ordinary 2.5-block reach with collision line-of-sight checks; higher plants need existing safe access. Farm preview bounds include this working height. Orchard behavior is unchanged. Standing positions include dry banks one block up and floors beneath reachable pods/vines. Cactus and sweet berry bushes are explicitly excluded by shared companion hazard navigation. Companions do not need damage immunity or water walking.

Special-crop picking uses the existing general interaction clip at 4 JE/t for 80 ticks (320 JE), no tool wear or hoe speed bonus. Rice keeps ordinary hoe harvest animation, timing and durability. Planting retains existing garden timing. Hauling effort scales with physical load. Produce feeds existing food, crafting and machine chains without growth acceleration, increased yields or new progression gates.

## Conservation, persistence and server cost

All products must fit in real cargo before block changes. Native sweet/glow berry interaction loot and column/cocoa block loot retain datapack behavior. Cocoa's replant bean is removed from drops or transactionally from unreserved cargo. Column harvest checks each affected block, permissions and loaded state; changes are applied top-down with shape updates suppressed, rolling back earlier block changes and cargo on failure, then notifying neighbors after commit. This prevents falling upper blocks from creating duplicate ground drops. Cranberry water stays in the plant's native fluid state; rice removal restores a water block. Work completion rechecks the actual crop and support, and uses existing per-cell worker claims.

The rolling discovery window remains 32 positions, with a 32-position slice at most once a second and at most four approach searches per pass. Existing search/path admission budgets apply. Columns examine at most sixteen stalks plus their top neighbor at completion. Missing neighboring chunks prevent placement/support probes. No global per-tick crop scan is added. Large zones take longer to discover overhead positions. Learned planting sites are capped to 256 per zone, saved by current assignment row and keyed by full target in memory so reordering preserves them. Old saves learn existing plants gradually. Sites and cargo are separate; remembered states are never real items or drops.
