# Armor designs of 8 October: Berserker, Paladin, Templar, Sentinel, Frost Knight, Wight King, Reaper, Banana and Scarab

Status: implemented on `claude/epic-pascal-f8gpfg`, after the four designs of the day before ([four-armor-designs.md](four-armor-designs.md)) and stacked with them on `claude/pharaoh-armor` (#246, itself on #241 and #240), since the sets share the lists they are added to. **Not yet compiled, game-tested or played:** the Java compiles only in CI, which has not run on this branch.
Proposal issue: none. On 8 October 2026 the owner sent three more designs of their own ("Those are fantastic lets do these 3 next and I'll upload more that I want in here as well", then "I made these 3"): a Blockbench sheet of a horned red-and-white set, worn and piece by piece, and two renders each of a white knight with purple cloth and of a dark knight with red cloth. The brief is the one they gave the day before: "just create the armors for the modpack and make them usable in game", crafting and the rest "decided later". While these were being built they added: "Make sure you are making complex models for the armor using the most of what our mod has to offer really capturing the crazy unique geometry of each armor", so every shape below is built as its own box, plate or relief rather than painted on a flat one. Later the same day they sent two more, three renders of a gold-and-black knight with a sword and a star-shaped shield and two of a white knight crowned with ice with a glowing ice sword: "Just made these ones aswell want them done weapons too please". Their two swords and the Sentinel's shield are arms of the owner's armor sets ([arms-vii.md](arms-vii.md#the-sentinels-and-the-frost-knights-arms)). Between those two messages they sent five pictures without words: two renders of a slate knight crowned with icicles and antlers, holding a long sword; a hooded reaper with white crescents about it; a banana costume; and a gold-and-blue Egyptian set beside its four icons. All four are built here, as the Wight King (with its sword), the Reaper, the Banana and the Scarab (the Egyptian set, its icons the owner's own). The Reaper was first built with its crescents read as part of the armor; on seeing it the owner wrote: "Umm the Reaper one needs to be redone... He is supposed to be holding 2 short scythe weapons they arent part of the armor and in general you just did that one really low quality", so its armor was rebuilt from the picture and the crescents became its weapon, the Reaper Scythe, one for each hand.

Owner: jimbozoomer-byte (design: the owner; implementation: Claude Opus 5.5).
Target milestone and tier: nine new armor-only tiers beside the owner's other nine ([bloodthorn-armor.md](bloodthorn-armor.md) and the sets after it). Their place in the game, and how they are obtained, are the owner's to decide later; until then they are creative-only.
Primary specialty and supported player role: combat (defense). Everyone who wears armor.

**The names are placeholders.** The pictures carry no names, so each set is named for its look: Berserker (the horned set), Paladin (the white knight), Templar (the dark knight), Sentinel (the gold-and-black guard), Frost Knight (the white knight crowned with ice) Wight King (the slate knight crowned with icicles and antlers, its face a black skull), Reaper (the hooded figure with the two short scythes), Banana (the costume) and Scarab (the gold-and-lapis Egyptian set; the owner's Pharaoh Armor, [pharaoh-armor.md](pharaoh-armor.md), is another set). The arms take their sets' names. Nothing has been released, so renaming one now is a find-and-replace and a regeneration; once released, the IDs stay.

## Player experience
Each set is worn as a 3D model on the knight armor's toolkit ([knight-armor.md](knight-armor.md)), built from the owner's pictures part for part, and has four 16 × 16 icons of its own. The pictures show the front and a little of one side; the backs are drawn in each design's own words. Preview them with `python3 tools/armor_preview.py --set <set>` (`berserker`, `paladin`, `templar`, `sentinel`, `frost_knight`, `wight_king`, `reaper`, `banana`, `scarab`).

### Their weapons
- **Sentinel Longsword** (`jugcraft:sentinel_longsword`): a broad gold blade pale along its edge, a straight gold crossguard with square ends set with a jet square, a brown grip, a gold pommel with a jet stone. It fights as a longsword, and its boon is **Mark** (a struck foe glows for 4 s).
- **Frost Knight Greatsword** (`jugcraft:frost_knight_greatsword`): a long blade of glowing ice, a crossguard of white frost flaring into jagged spikes with an ice gem, a white grip and an ice pommel. It fights as a greatsword, and its boon is **Frost** (Slowness II, 3 s).
- **Wight King Zweihander** (`jugcraft:wight_king_zweihander`): the long sword the Wight King holds, a slate blade pale along its edges and grooved dark down its middle, a crossguard of jagged slate shards swept toward the point with a glowing cyan gem at its heart, a dark grip and a slate pommel set with a cyan stone. It fights as a zweihander, and its boon is **Drain** (each hit heals its wielder half a heart), as a wight's would.
- **Reaper Scythe** (`jugcraft:reaper_scythe`): the short scythe the Reaper holds in each hand: a crescent of five bone-white links sweeping out from the head and round to a point, each a white frame round a slate hollow, every other one set a little further out as the owner's zig-zag; a short bone-white handle wrapped in slate, a slate collar and butt. It fights as a kama (quick hooking cuts, and it clears brush), and its boon is **Wither** (Wither, 3 s). One item: carry one in each hand, as drawn (the off hand's is for show; vanilla strikes with the main hand).
- All four are Arms VII set arms ([arms-vii.md](arms-vii.md#the-sentinels-and-the-frost-knights-arms)): epic, twice as hard-wearing as steel, a 3D model in the hand and a 16×16 icon of their own, creative only.
- **Sentinel Shield** (`jugcraft:sentinel_shield`): the owner's four-pointed star of gold, built in 3D as the kit's shields are (52 boxes): the star stepped out in half-pixel rows, a raised pale gold diamond frame on it, a dark recess inside the frame and a chequered gold diamond at its heart, raised again, with a pale stone at its middle; gold at its edges, dark boards and a brown strap behind. It blocks exactly as a steel heater shield does (raised in 0.1 s, 90 degrees either side) and, like the swords, is epic, lasts twice as long (1800) and is creative only. Its tooltip names its trait, **Quick Raise**, and its set.

## Connections
- Existing input producer: none yet (creative only). The repair items come from vanilla (quartz, amethyst shards, netherite ingots, gold ingots, blue ice, packed ice, bone, yellow wool, lapis lazuli).
- Existing output consumer: the player's armor slots; each piece enchants, trims and equips as any armor of its slot (it is in `#minecraft:head_armor` and the other slot tags).
- Technology connection: none yet. Magic connection: none yet.
- Reachable entry path: not yet, by the owner's decision. Nothing requires these sets, so nothing is blocked on them.
- Which connections are required vs optional: all optional.

## Balance and automation
- **At or above netherite,** but for the Banana (iron's defense, a costume), so they must come late. Their recipes or drops are the owner's to set, and should cost at least a netherite set's worth, whatever mends them.
- **No conversion loops:** nothing turns them back into materials.
- **Starting numbers,** not played.

## Multiplayer and persistence
- **Plain armor items:** nothing new is saved, and the server handles them as any armor (`JugcraftGear.armorTier`).
- **The 3D models are client-side only** (`client/WornModelLayer`), drawn from `assets/jugcraft/worn_models.json` (78 new entries; none of the earlier ones changed) and the atlases `textures/entity/equipment/3d/<set>.png`.
- **IDs:** `jugcraft:<set>_helmet`, `_chestplate`, `_leggings` and `_boots` for `berserker`, `paladin`, `templar`, `sentinel`, `frost_knight`, `wight_king`, `reaper`, `banana` and `scarab`, the repair tags `jugcraft:repairs_<set>_gear`, the swords `jugcraft:sentinel_longsword`, `jugcraft:frost_knight_greatsword` and `jugcraft:wight_king_zweihander`, and the shield `jugcraft:sentinel_shield`. Placeholder names (above) until the owner confirms them; stable once released.
- **The swords** are ordinary items: their boons are worked on the server when they strike (`ArmItem`), as every Arms VII arm's are. **The shield** blocks through vanilla's blocks-attacks component, worked on the server, as the kit's shields do.
- **No equipment asset files:** every piece is drawn in 3D, so no `equipment/<set>.json` is written.

## The shared parts it uses and adds
- Each set: one `ARMOR_TIERS` entry, one Java material and one `armorTier(...)` call, a row in `ArmorTiersGameTests`, a palette and four icon maps; its module in `armor_models.SET_MODULES` (the Paladin and the Templar share one). `ArmorTiersClientGameTests` shows every tier, so it shoots these with no change.
- The two painters above.
- **Arms VII:** three set lines (`sentinel`, `frost_knight`, `wight_king`) and their arms in `tools/arms_variants.py` and `ArmVariants`, their designs and materials in `tools/arms_variants_art.py`, three icon maps in `tools/arms_icons/`. `ArmsVIIClientGameTests` holds them from the front, and moves the patterns' frames to the bottom row of its wall.
- **A set's shield** (new): `SET_SHIELDS` and `SHIELD_SHAPES` in `tools/arms_variants.py` and `ArmVariants.SET_SHIELDS`, registered as an `ArmShieldItem` (which now names an armor set's shield's set) with `JugcraftArms.shield`'s numbers for its base kind; the star's geometry in `tools/arms_kit.py` (`star_elements`, `star_part`) and its painting in `tools/arms_kit_art.py` (`star_face`; the kit's back and trim painters now take their materials, so the kit's own shields draw as before). `ArmsVIIClientGameTests` shoots it beside the longsword and raised.

## Dependencies and assets
- No new dependencies.
- The designs are the owner's; their pictures are not committed. Every model, texture and icon here is drawn by code from them: the eight modules, the nine palettes, `tools/armor_icons/<set>/` (the Scarab's transcribed from the owner's own icons), the three swords' designs and their icon maps, the shield's model and paint. Nothing is copied or traced from any mod or from vanilla, and no pixel of the owner's pictures is in the textures.
- The owner's asset library ([art/owner-library/README.md](../../art/owner-library/README.md)) was searched first, as for the four sets before: none of its armor is these designs, so nothing was taken from it.

## Rollout and open questions
- **Names:** the five names, and so the swords', are placeholders (above).
- **How they are obtained,** and **their numbers,** as the owner decides; until then creative-only.
- **The Reaper Scythe** is one item; the owner's figure holds one in each hand, so a player carries two, the off hand's for show (vanilla strikes with the main hand only). It fights as a kama; the owner may want its own numbers, or the two to differ.
- **Weapons not built:** those in the Paladin's and the Templar's renders (a sword and a kite shield; a dark clawed axe), which were not asked for when those sets were sent.
- **The Sentinel's shield** blocks as a heater shield does; the owner may want its own numbers. Its back is our guess (the renders show little of it): dark boards and a strap, as the kit's shields have.
- **What is open by design:** the Berserker's face, the back of its head under the cap and its upper arms; the Banana's face, arms and feet; the Scarab's face and forearms; the Frost Knight's arms below its pauldrons (the owner's renders show their model's plain pale blue there). On an armor stand those parts show the stand.
- **Where the models differ from the designs** (each can be changed):
  - **Berserker:** the back of the cap is drawn plain (the sheet shows only the front); the jaw frame's back posts and side bars are guesses at what the front view hides.
  - **Paladin and Templar:** their paint is our reading of the renders, white on the Paladin and lighter slate on the Templar than the owner's, whose plates read darker (the renders are lit, and the texel patterns differ in places); lifting the texels from the renders did not come out clean enough to keep, as the figures stand differently in each. The backs are guesses in the designs' words (a back plate with a spine, flaps behind the cloth); the Templar's crest is three straight pieces where the owner's curls; the shield on the chest steps where the owner's curves.
  - **Sentinel:** rebuilt from the owner's three renders ([blockbench-armor.md](blockbench-armor.md)): the arms are built as the renders pose them (the right arm 10 degrees and the left 37 degrees forward, holding the sword and the shield), so standing with the arms down the pauldron's upper plate rises a little more steeply than drawn. Faces no render shows (the inside of the arms, the soles, parts hidden behind the sword and the shield) are filled from the faces opposite. The wearer audit shows 0.00 model px² of the wearer standing, walking and sneaking from four views.
  - **Frost Knight:** rebuilt from the owner's front render ([blockbench-armor.md](blockbench-armor.md)); their render from behind is softer and the figure stands turned in it, so it was used to check the model by eye, not to lift texels: the back of the helm is painted (the ice cross on mottled white) and what the front render does not show (the cuirass's back, the arms, the drape) keeps the earlier paint and shapes. The frost the front render shows is a set of cut-out sheets facing the front, carrying its texels; worn armor is drawn from both sides, so from behind the sheets show their art mirrored, where the owner's Blockbench view hides them. The drape behind the right shoulder and the feathers down the right arm remain our reading of the white mass the renders show there (the arm raised); the bits of ice floating round the knight were taken as the scene, not the armor.
  - **Wight King:** the antlers are four straight bars each, bending only where they meet, where the owner's taper smoothly; the right shoulder's jagged plates are four flat shards on a block; the back of the helm and the right side are drawn from the front view; the glow round the knight and the bits of ice floating round it were taken as the scene, not the armor.
  - **Reaper:** the robe's skirt is split per leg into strips, as every robe on this toolkit is, so the legs can swing; the pouch rides the left thigh, so it swings with it. Its hood and arms keep their painted texels: the render's hood is the owner's but the bars and the clasp are too small to lift cleanly from one view, and the arms are hidden behind the scythes there. The strap and the cages are our reading of the render's diagonal band and white frames (it shows them at an angle, the arms raised). The back is drawn in the design's words. The scythes' links are flat in the hand (a 3D model of the design's shapes, each with its own thickness), where the owner's are open frames.
  - **Banana:** the owner's is one long banana; here the part below the waist is split per leg so the legs can swing, and the brown end rides the right boot. The arms and the feet are left bare, as drawn.
  - **Scarab:** the owner's kilt is one piece round both legs; here it is split per leg, so the legs can swing. The right side and the back are drawn from the render's front and left back view.
- **Known limits of the 3D layer,** as for every set: trims are kept but not drawn on the 3D models; babies wear nothing visible; mobs with odd proportions (a zombie villager's head, a skeleton's thin limbs) wear them loosely.
