# Companion production stock targets

Owner-requested implementation with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 25 October 2026. Extends the existing Filter, Supply/Output, physical eight-slot cargo, companion work scheduling and native recipes. Initial scope: Cooking Pot, Hearth Oven and Cutting Board. Extended to Skillet, Kitchen Stove, vanilla Furnace/Smoker/Blast Furnace/Campfires, Workshop Bench/Small Ceramic Kiln/Small Crucible Furnace, standard item-recipe processors, vanilla Crafter and Auto-Crafter. Remaining-station coverage adds Cider Press/Barrel, Candy/Canning Kettles, Punch Bowl, Spinning Wheel, Brewing Stand chains, Alchemy Crucible, Electroplating Bath and industrial item-producing recipes. No new items, recipes, dependencies, currencies or machine speed bonuses.

## Player controls

Open a companion's Jobs page and the workstation's funnel **Filter** button. Put finished products into the existing 3 by 3 ghost grid. In supported station editors, empty-hand left-click selects a product for editing; right-click clears it. Real cursor items are never consumed. Other station and porter Filters keep their current controls.

Each distinct product defaults to **Unlimited**. Switch to **Maintain stock** for an initial target of 64 and restart threshold of 32. Set **Target** (1–4096 items, not stacks) and **Restock at** (0 through Target minus one). The arrow buttons apply a one-item change; typed quantities use **Apply quantities** or Enter. A new target starts a replenishment cycle. After reaching its target, it waits until stock reaches the restart threshold before refilling. Set Unlimited to remove a product's limit; clearing its last ghost also removes its limit. Duplicate ghosts refer to the same product goal. An empty Filter retains automatic available-recipe behavior.

Targets and the counted-container list belong to the workstation and are shared by its helpers. If no counted Outputs have been chosen yet, enabling a target copies that job's linked Outputs. **Use linked Outputs** explicitly replaces the common list with the editing companion's current job links; changing another helper's links does not silently change the common stock pool. Counted containers are listed with their existing sign/custom names and coordinates. Each worker still needs permission and a permitted Output route into that pool. Configure a subset with Supplies > Job links before adopting it. Supply ingredients are not counted as finished stock.

The panel shows stored products separately from goods at the station or on the way, including projected products from committed ingredients. A target is a replenishment goal: complete recipe batches and earned uniform bonus products can exceed it. No output is deleted or split to enforce an exact cap.

## Work and accounting

Cooking Pot delivery planning bounds balanced ingredient batches by the missing output quantity, including ingredients installed and carried by registered helpers. Its existing bulk-cooking/unloading behavior remains. Recipe selection favors available products furthest below their goals proportionally; unrestricted products remain eligible when limited products are covered or unavailable. Existing partial/committed batches finish before switching recipes.

The Hearth Oven still holds one pie or cake. A companion reserves the raw food during its physical Supply trip, and can fetch fuel for an already loaded food even when the target is met. Tending and rescue of finished food retain priority. Cutting Boards reserve one raw input and retain all results and byproducts of a completed cut. Both preserve native yields, capacity, processing, effort and tool durability.

Skillets, Stove hobs and vanilla cooking use their actual native recipe result and installed raw food. Managed finished buffers are counted separately. Furnaces retain separate fuel demand; satisfying a product target does not strand already loaded food without heat. Recipe selection favors an available understocked product, then Unlimited products, and keeps the chosen plan while ingredients are loaded or in transit.

Dual-purpose furnace items (for example, logs smelted into charcoal and also burned as fuel) use separate recipe-slot limits during simulated and physical insertion. Fuel does not fill the remaining native eight-item ingredient allowance and accidentally exceed a smaller stock target. Inbound estimates exclude the extra fuel from promised charcoal.

Live validation exposed an existing campfire compatibility problem with the pack's Lithium optimization: direct slot writes left an empty campfire's ticker asleep. Committed companion placements now invoke vanilla `placeFood` hooks for the newly loaded slots. Transaction probes restore the insertion mask and inventory and do not invoke placement effects. This uses native hooks with or without Lithium, without a dependency on its internal API or a separate cooking ticker.

Starter workshops count both queued ingredients and the native work lane's committed results. The lane consumes ingredients at the start; its output is therefore counted until completion without inventing a second recipe or inventory. Retained tools/ceramics and fuel remain real Supply deliveries. Limited fuel requests account for remaining work and heat credit. Standard processors use their existing cached recipe plans and native input/output slots, with balanced ingredient quantities and whole-batch yields. Random byproducts count once produced, not as guaranteed future stock. Side configuration, Auto/On/Off transport detection, locks, helper caps, energy effort and native processing remain authoritative.

The standard processor adapter covers ordinary single/multiple ingredient recipes and legacy fluid recipes with real item inputs and results; fluids still use native pipes. Ordinary processors need an item recipe catalog to offer these controls. Delivery cancellation records whether a physical manifest was registered, so unloaded-station cleanup works for the added station families without scanning blocks or creating cleanup entries for unrelated jobs.

### Remaining recipe stations

The ordinary vanilla Crafting Table also supports these goals through [companion crafting-table work](companion-crafting-table.md). Its selected native recipe supplies an exact ingredient pattern to the shared crafting-stock accountant; staged and inbound ingredients count by slot and component identity, while products and container remainders use the existing physical Output route. Its manual work and energy cost are recorded separately from the two automatic Crafters.

The final extension, also implemented with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), covers Cider Press, Cider Barrel, Candy Kettle, Canning Kettle, Punch Bowl, Spinning Wheel, Brewing Stand, Alchemy Crucible, Electroplating Bath and shared industrial item processors. It adds Filter access to the four fixed-workflow stations that previously lacked it (Press, Canning, Punch and Spinning), preserving their existing native inputs, outputs, transactions and progression. No new items, currencies, recipes, energy costs, worker loops or dependencies are introduced.

Native press accounting excludes juice already released from the pulp, avoiding duplicate projected cider. Barrels reserve incoming sweet cider and count aging servings once; a chosen aging stage persists. Candy plans only enough sugar for the missing whole-piece yield and keeps staged/heating/tray batches intact. Punch preserves whole berry yields. Canning counts sealed preserve outputs separately from raw/spoiled jars, with reusable water separate from production. Spinning preserves the wool color and whole yarn yield.

Brewing plans cap the initial bottle count to the missing final potion quantity and favor understocked available chains. Later reagents/fuel still finish a loaded or reserved batch even after the target changes. Exact potion contents distinguish target rules and shared Output counts. Alchemy reserves the written formula's whole dose yield, finishes its remaining ingredients and containers, and does not treat passive water-only refill as another committed production order. Idle Draught/Salve selection favors the allowed shortage; its selected form is saved. Native research, formula, heat and water behavior remain in place.

Industrial item recipes use the existing selected plan, item inputs, real output slots and native escrowed lane results. Companion replenishment targets coexist with native automation-card caps, recipe locks, pause controls and fluid requirements. Repeated ingredients fill complete recipe layers before the next layer. Stock-capable product catalogs are cached against native recipe-catalog identity. Fluid products remain on pipes with native machine targets: bucket ghost slots are read-only for companion stock settings. Electroplating retains its procedural native plan; goals count plated tools of the selected item type, ignoring unplated tools in counted storage. Coating variants share the existing item-type Filter identity.

Rules now save a sanitized item icon instead of an item identifier alone. Existing identifier-only rules load unchanged. Identity components are explicitly limited to potion contents for brewing, yarn dye color for spinning, and the sealed flag for canning; custom names, freshness timestamps and arbitrary stack components are not accepted as new rule dimensions. Counts still share the same 20-tick bounded container snapshot, physical carrier registry, permissions and unloaded-storage behavior. Normal item-only Filters retain their prior matching behavior.

Both Crafters use their existing player-configured grid and native crafting recipe result. Stock settings never choose a different crafting pattern. The vanilla Crafter's saved template remains available to edit its product target after the real grid empties; disabled slots, exact item components and complete-pattern checks are retained. Deliveries fill one layer across the required slots before a second layer so a one-batch target cannot strand repeated ingredients in one slot. The Auto-Crafter retains one real item per occupied slot as before; these pattern items are excluded from projected products. Its existing two-items-per-slot delivery cap remains.

Crafting reservations count complete per-slot batches, including inbound physical ingredients. Surplus in one slot cannot stand in for a missing copy in another slot. Whole recipe yields can exceed a target, and native container remainders are still unloaded through Output. Vanilla redstone pulses and Auto-Crafter power/redstone settings remain required. Already installed or committed supplies may finish after a goal edit. No additional processing ticker, crafting buffer, recipe cache or inventory scan is introduced; pattern checks inspect at most nine slots and use native recipe lookup.

Counting includes the common Outputs (double-chest aliases once), completed station results, real outbound cargo, and expected products of loaded/inbound inputs. Pickup revalidates current stock before taking supplies. Existing inputs and already collected products can finish their journeys. Stock targets do not block player operation or independently supplied native processing; players and external automation can produce additional goods. These targets coordinate helpers at one workstation; independently configured workstations are not a global shared production order.

Changing/removing an endpoint, locking a counted store, missing/unloaded chunks or a count exceeding the supported bounded inventory size pauses new replenishment with **Stock unavailable**. A missing count is never interpreted as zero. Output collection remains available. A helper that dies or is deliberately removed releases its delivery registration; deferred cleanup is saved if its workstation is unloaded. Chunk unloading retains the registration, and new production conservatively waits until the registered helper becomes available to verify its physical cargo. Reordering jobs does not move workstation goals to another station.

## Authority, persistence and server cost

The existing server validates owner/party access, distance, exact open menu, station identity, live claims/locks, slot/value bounds, revision and edit rate. Ghost items cannot become real inventory. Numeric edits do not reset native recipe progress. The client receives changed-only, bounded snapshots while the Filter menu is open. Existing slots, assignment IDs and status ordinals are preserved; stock statuses append.

Additive `ProductionStock` data on the station's existing filter stores up to nine item rules, four counted endpoints, the refill latch and at most 64 registered physical deliveries. The normal saved companion transport manifest remains the source of truth for loaded helpers; registrations reconcile without global entity searches. Old saves default to Unlimited. Matching uses the same item identity as the supported station Filters; timestamps or custom names do not turn identical food into separate targets.

One station shares its inventory snapshot across helpers for up to 20 ticks. Pickup invalidates it for a fresh bounded count. Counts inspect at most 128 views per distinct assigned container, up to four containers; oversized storage pauses limited production. No neighborhood chest scans, chunk loading, offline simulation or per-tick inventory scans are added. Native machine contents and current delivery manifests are checked separately from the cached chest counts.
