# Fonta59 architectural adaptations in Frostward College

Fonta59 created [Winterhold (Skyrim TES) with interior](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/winterhold-skyrim-tes-with-interior/). The Jugcraft owner reports direct permission to selectively reuse and modify portions of his Minecraft world, provided the entire map is not simply copied and pasted. The owner-supplied `Winterhold_by_fonta59.rar` was inspected locally; neither the archive nor its unmodified world is part of this repository or mod distribution.

## Monumental Hall interior section (22 October 2026)

The 23 October Hall-only continuation studies the same bounded donor section's sequence of occupied annulus, deep open well, thick piers and alternating full/slab/stair courses. In `tools/academy_hall_masterwork.py` those relationships are redrawn as four new high vault ribs and occupied dark coffers within Frostward's existing Y38–50 teaching wells; they are paired with original asymmetric gallery cabinets, an apse console, distinct research pockets and a carved south frontage. The donor's block coordinates, complete room plan and world are not copied. New in-game visual review is pending.

Two actual Minecraft reviews of the new Hall showed that the first hanging square and second heavy copper meridians obscured its height. The third pass keeps the donor-inspired open annular section and strengthens it with newly proportioned lower Nordic stone arches, a dark coffer beneath Frostward's occupied bridge, and a thin four-lantern iron instrument. The stage and south-tower heraldic relief are original Frostward work. These changes remain unaccepted until the next Minecraft capture.

The [third runtime Hall review](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/actions/runs/39953563493/artifacts/12501787560) confirms the arches and lighter instrument, while showing a small stage and a south screen that missed most of the curved wall. The next original Frostward revision raises a nine-wide demonstration deck with its own apparatus and fits the framed heraldic screen to each bay's actual outer stone. The [fourth in-game capture](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/actions/runs/40011711693/artifacts/12526373680) confirms the full framed tower panel, raised deck, ribbed coffers and inhabited gallery at wide and close range. The Hall-only milestone is visually accepted with its prominent central bridge band recorded as a limit. No donor blocks or coordinates were transplanted; the modified open-section and pier/rib grammar were adapted to Frostward's existing larger Hall.

The new Hall pass studies the bounded donor Hall at world **X18–67, Z−207..−153, Y116–145**. At Y118 its stepped slab/stair annulus encloses an open teaching void; Y129–135 turns that void into a load-bearing upper ring through full stone, thin slabs and stair courses. Frostward selectively adapts this **sectional relationship**, not those source coordinates or a whole room. Its existing Y38 gallery now has a guarded three-wide east–west crossing, larger open north/south teaching wells and the same central anchor, perimeter route and original stairs. New original tuff piers, rising stone vaults, high timber/deepslate coffers, a bronze/amethyst armillary, two-tier north teaching apse and three-level scholarly seating fit Frostward's larger Y25–50 occupied volume. The Fonta59 world remains outside the repo. Runtime visual acceptance is pending the new close and wide Minecraft captures.

This checkpoint keeps Frostward's existing **176 × 161** campus footprint, seven-landmark arrangement, 45 room/anchor points, academy stations, Arctus Vale, S01 runes and Midden. The generated structure has grown from **76 to 88 blocks high** for the Hall's stronger bridge silhouette. The single source remains `tools/winterhold_layout.json` and its Python generator passes.

## Current evidence and limits

The regenerated template has **339,356 cells, 91 palette states and size 176 × 88 × 161**. Focused Python structure tests pass **13/13**; the Node template verifier passes **45/45 named points**, eight doors, 16 bed halves, eight signs and zero snow, with 16,438 reachable standing positions after the open gate revision. The regenerated plan, bridge projection and overview in `docs/images/` are computed from actual NBT cells. These are geometry previews. [Minecraft client GameTest at the gate revision](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/actions/runs/39652194027/artifacts/12369837165) passed with 16 screenshots; its bridge, near-gate and court log previews show the adapted massing and autumn trees, with the Hall more exposed through the opened gate. The earlier full-resolution [autumn runtime views](../images/frostward-runtime-151/overview.png) remain in the branch. [Full Build at the corrected server-test head](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/actions/runs/39654583631) passed, including the 2,230-test optional-integrations-absent server run and all client shards. Two-client autumn controls, human interactions and performance checks remain to be recorded in the progress ledger.

The donor world's stair cores and additional chambers have been cataloged only at a coarse level. Further reuse needs bounded extraction, conversion and gameplay review. No complete donor building or map region has been committed. This project also preserves the three owner-supplied Skyrim/Minecraft references as the controlling landmark and scale references.
