# Pumpkin Night

Status: implemented. CI builds it and runs its game tests and client screenshots (below); not yet played by hand, and the Farm Stand needs a two-client playtest before it is called done.
Proposal issue: none. The owner asked for it directly on 4 October 2026 ("Lets come up with another 20 very detailed well thought out additions to add for the halloween / fall season using the first 3 images for ideas of prop sets … lets do them piece by piece maybe 5 at a time"). These are prop sets 16–20 of the [Witching Season plan](witching-season.md), drawn from the reference pictures' red and green pumpkins, pumpkins on display, curling stems, the scarecrow in a tattered cloak, and the jack-o'-lanterns.
Owner: @jimbozoomer-byte
Target milestone and tier: Discovery tier. Every recipe takes early materials (pumpkins, jack-o'-lanterns, a note block, planks, wool, a chest, hay bales, sticks, string, vines, leaves, clay, gold nuggets, amethyst shards). The Farm Stand trades in Jugs, the walled town's existing currency.
Primary specialty and supported player role: farming and trade, with building and redstone pieces. The heirlooms and the Farm Stand let a grower sell their harvest to neighbours; the effigy and the choir are a fall night's festivities.

## Player experience

### 16. Red Kuri and Kabocha pumpkins
- **Red Kuri** (a smooth, deep red-orange pumpkin) and **Kabocha** (squat and dark green, flecked and streaked pale) join the White, Jarrahdale and Cinderella heirlooms.
  - **Seeds**: from short grass (they share the grass-seed chance with every other Jugcraft seed), from wild patches (Red Kuri in taiga and spooky biomes, Kabocha in forests and jungles), from the Halloween Peddler (an emerald for two), or four from a pumpkin in a crafting grid.
  - They grow on stems like the other heirlooms.
- **They carve**: the Carving Knife carves each into its own hand-carved pumpkin. Lit, a carved Red Kuri glows a deep ember orange and a carved Kabocha a greenish gold; every other carved pumpkin keeps its candle glow.
- **They cook**: **Red Kuri Soup** (a bowl, a Red Kuri, an onion and garlic; 9 hunger, 0.7 saturation) and **Kabocha Tempura** (a Kabocha, wheat and an egg; two portions of 6 hunger, 0.8 saturation) in the Cooking Pot. Both make pumpkin pie like the other heirlooms.
- **They count as pumpkins** for scarecrow and Hay Golem heads, costumes, and the Pumpkin Chunkin' Trebuchet. A Red Kuri flies at 0.99 of a pumpkin's speed (1.05 carved), a Kabocha at 0.96 (1.02 carved).

### 17. The Farm Stand
- **Farm Stand** (two blocks wide; orange and white wool, planks, a chest and a hay bale): a trestle of weathered boards with a stepped riser at the back.
  - Six slatted crates sit on it, three on the table and three on the riser.
  - A striped orange-and-cream awning on four posts slopes to a scalloped valance.
  - A slate header board stands over the awning's front, and each crate carries a slate price tag.
  - There is a hay bale and pumpkins under the table and a bundle of ornamental corn on a post.
  - It faces whoever places it, its second block to their right.
- **Whoever places it owns it.** The owner's name is chalked on the header board.
- **Use it to open its screen**: the six crates, each with its price in Jugs under it, your own Jugs, and your inventory.
  - **The owner** puts goods into the crates (a stack each) and takes them out.
  - The owner picks a crate with its **Price** button and moves its price up or down by 1, 10, 100 or 1,000 Jugs. A price runs from 1 Jug to the town's maximum balance.
  - **Anyone else** sees the goods and prices and buys **one item at a time** with the crate's **Buy** button. The price leaves the buyer's Jugs and goes straight to the owner, even while the owner is offline.
  - Buyers can't put anything in or take anything out by hand.
- **In the world**, the crates show their goods (one to three of each, heaped by how many are left) and each tag shows the crate's price, chalked.
- **Only the owner takes it down** (or an operator in creative). Anyone else who tries is told whose stand it is, and it stands. Taken down, it drops once and spills what its crates held.
  - Explosions and pistons don't move it.

### 18. The garlands
- **Pumpkin Vine Garland** (a vine, a pumpkin and string): two green vines twisting round each other with broad pumpkin leaves hanging either side, and little pumpkins and green gourds every block and a half.
- **Autumn Leaf Garland** (maple, aspen and chestnut leaves and string): a twine cord hung thick with red, orange, gold and rust leaves.
- Both hang between **String Light Hooks** exactly as the Jack-o'-Lantern String Lights do: use the garland on one hook and then another, up to sixteen blocks apart. They sag and chain from hook to hook, and a sneaking empty hand takes one down.
- Both carry tiny warm bulbs that glow whenever either hook is lit, as the string lights do.

### 19. The Harvest Effigy
- **Harvest Effigy** (three blocks tall; three hay bales, sticks and string): a wicker man on a straw-bound post.
  - His legs are bound in straw, his body is woven wicker under a straw shoulder roll, and his arms are a cross-pole sleeved in straw with tufts for hands.
  - He stands in a ring of corn sheaves on a straw mound and wears a tattered cloak, green until dyed.
  - It faces whoever places it.
- **Dress him**:
  - Any carved pumpkin (anything a scarecrow wears) goes on as his head; an empty hand takes it back.
  - A dye recolours his whole cloak.
- **Burn him on a fall night.** Flint and steel, a fire charge, a torch or a soul torch lights him.
  - Not by day ("It will only catch at night") and not with rain on him ("It's too wet to burn").
  - He burns for 30 seconds in a roaring blaze that climbs from the corn at his feet to over his head, his hands flaring.
  - Sparks and smoke rise, and crows within 24 blocks scatter.
  - No fire blocks are placed and nothing catches.
  - Rain puts him out, and he can be lit again.
- **Harvest Cheer**: every player within 16 blocks of him while he burns gets Regeneration I for 30 seconds and Luck for 5 minutes, **once per player per night**, however many effigies burn.
- **Burnt through**, he falls into **Effigy Ashes** (a smouldering drift with charred sticks and embers) and drops his head.
  - The ashes, shovelled or dug, give **2 Hearth Ash**.
  - **Hearth Ash** is a weak fertilizer: used on a crop, it gives every crop in the 3 × 3 patch round it a dose of bone meal (bat guano's rule).

### 20. The Singing Pumpkins
- **Singing Pumpkin** in four voices, each a jack-o'-lantern of its own shape and colour with a carved face glowing from inside (light 12):
  - **Bass**: a wide, squat, deep orange pumpkin with heavy carved brows and a thick stem (a jack-o'-lantern, a note block and planks);
  - **Tenor**: a round orange pumpkin with a curling leaf (with clay);
  - **Alto**: a tall golden pumpkin with a purple ribbon bow (with a gold nugget);
  - **Soprano**: a small, pale ghost pumpkin with lashes and a curly tendril (with an amethyst shard).
- **They sing.** Like a note block, each is tuned by use over 25 notes, two octaves round its voice's middle note: up, or down while sneaking, wrapping round. It sings its note when tuned (and says which note, F#0 to F#2) and on each rising redstone signal.
  - Singing, its mouth opens into a round glowing O for a second and closes, its eyes widen, and its pupils roll up.
  - Idle, its pupils wander round its eyes.
  - Wire a row to a sequencer and they sing a tune together.
- **Their voices are Jugcraft's own**, two sung vowels ("ah" and "oh") per voice, synthesised by `tools/choir_sounds.py`. The middle notes are F#3 (bass), F#4 (tenor and alto, the alto brighter) and F#5 (soprano).

## Changes from the plan
- **The Farm Stand** is worked through a screen rather than by sneak-using it, and takes a hay bale where the plan had a sign.
  - The owner stocks the crates in the screen and sets prices with buttons.
  - Buying is one item a click.
  - The server takes one purchase per player every 4 ticks.
- **The Autumn Leaf Garland** takes chestnut leaves where the plan had larch: Jugcraft has no larch leaves to use.
- **Hearth Ash** works over a 3 × 3 patch with one dose each (bat guano's rule) rather than exactly as one bone meal.
- **The effigy** also lights from a fire charge or a soul torch.

## Connections
- Existing input producer: pumpkins, jack-o'-lanterns, note blocks, the heirloom seed routes (grass, wild patches, the Halloween Peddler), hay bales, string, sticks, vines, Jugcraft's maple, aspen and chestnut leaves, wool and dyes; Jugs from the walled town's shops and ATMs.
- Existing output consumer:
  - the Cooking Pot (the soup and tempura);
  - pumpkin pie (the heirloom tag);
  - the Carving Knife and carving screen;
  - scarecrows, Hay Golems and costumes (heads);
  - the trebuchet;
  - String Light Hooks and their power;
  - crows (they flee the blaze);
  - crops (Hearth Ash);
  - redstone (the choir);
  - Jugs (the Farm Stand moves them between players).
- Technology connection: redstone sequencing for the choir; the hooks' electricity lights the garlands. Magic connection: Harvest Cheer's luck.
- Reachable entry path: all need only early materials and a crafting table; Jugs are earned at the walled town's shops. None needs these props first.
- Required vs optional: everything is optional; nothing gates progression.

## Balance and automation
- **The Farm Stand creates nothing.** Every Jug a buyer pays goes to the owner (`Jugs.transfer`), and every item sold left a crate. It never takes items or Jugs from anyone else.
  - Prices run from 1 Jug to the town's maximum balance.
  - A sale that would carry the owner past that maximum is refused before anything moves.
- **The effigy is a loss.** Three hay bales (27 wheat), two sticks and two string give at most 2 Hearth Ash: 18 doses over two 3 × 3 patches, far less than the wheat is worth. Harvest Cheer is once per player per night, however many effigies burn.
- **Heirloom seeds** share the existing grass-seed chance (12.5 % of short grass drops one Jugcraft seed, shared among 27 kinds), so short grass gives no more seeds than before. Pumpkins give four seeds in a grid, as the other heirlooms do.
- **Recipes**: each takes materials in and gives the prop; breaking gives the prop back (the ashes give Hearth Ash instead). No loops.
- **Work done**:
  - A burning effigy checks every 20 ticks for rain and for players within 16 blocks, and is otherwise idle.
  - A singing pumpkin does nothing until used or powered.
  - The stand does nothing until someone presses a button.
  - The blaze, the faces, the heaped goods and the chalk are drawn by the client.

## Multiplayer and persistence
- **Server-side**: tuning, singing, lighting, cheering, extinguishing, collapsing, scooping, dressing and dyeing are decided on the server, and so is every Farm Stand action.
  - **The stand's screen only asks.** Each button is vanilla's container button packet, and the server checks it again:
    - the stand is still there and within 6 blocks of the player's eyes;
    - a price button is pressed by the owner;
    - the crate holds something;
    - the buyer isn't the owner and has the price;
    - the owner's purse has room;
    - no more than one purchase per player every 4 ticks.
  - The crate slots refuse anyone but the owner, on the server as on the client. Clients are never trusted about prices, ownership or balances; prices come down as menu data from the server.
  - **Breaking the stand** is checked on the server (the player-block-break event), and so is breaking the effigy's head off: only an empty hand takes it, and not while he burns.
- **Build rights**: lighting the effigy and scooping the ashes need the right to use blocks there.
- **Saved**:
  - block state: the choir's note, voice and power; the effigy's parts, light and cloak; the stand's parts;
  - block entities: the stand's owner (ID and name), its six crates and six prices; the effigy's head and when he was lit, so a burn carries on after a reload;
  - world data: the night each player was last cheered (`data/jugcraft_harvest_cheer.dat` in the Overworld), so leaving and coming back or a restart gives no second helping;
  - garlands as a hook's strand, by name.
- **Drops on breaking**: the stand spills its crates and drops once from its first block. The effigy drops once and his head drops with him. The ashes give Hearth Ash.
- **New IDs**:
  - blocks and items `farm_stand`, `harvest_effigy`, `effigy_ashes`, `singing_pumpkin_bass`, `singing_pumpkin_tenor`, `singing_pumpkin_alto`, `singing_pumpkin_soprano`, `red_kuri_pumpkin`, `kabocha_pumpkin`, `hand_carved_red_kuri_pumpkin`, `hand_carved_kabocha_pumpkin`, `red_kuri_pumpkin_stem`, `kabocha_pumpkin_stem` (and their attached stems);
  - items `pumpkin_vine_garland`, `autumn_leaf_garland`, `hearth_ash`, `red_kuri_pumpkin_seeds`, `kabocha_pumpkin_seeds`, `red_kuri_soup`, `kabocha_tempura`;
  - block entity types `farm_stand`, `harvest_effigy`, `singing_pumpkin`;
  - the menu `farm_stand`;
  - sound events `singing_pumpkin.bass`, `.tenor`, `.alto`, `.soprano` and `effigy.burn`;
  - the saved data `harvest_cheer`;
  - their recipes.
- **Feature switch**: the recipes follow the agriculture feature switch.

## Dependencies and assets
- **No new dependencies.** The garlands extend the existing String Light Hook (`StringLightHookBlockEntity.Strand`); the stand uses the town's `Jugs`.
- **Models**: built on `tools/flora_art.py` by `tools/decor20_data.py`, which paints their textures by code: 128 × 128 for the stand and the effigy, 64 × 64 for the rest.
- **Art style**: the creature props (faces, bones, fur, scales, fire) are painted with the clean, cute painters in `tools/cute_art.py` since 5 October 2026, after the owner asked for simpler, smoother creatures ([ART_DIRECTION.md](../ART_DIRECTION.md#creatures-and-faces-cute-and-clean)).
- **Client-drawn parts**:
  - the singing faces' mouth, eyes and pupils (`textures/entity/singing_pumpkin_face`, 32 × 32);
  - the effigy's cloak (`decor20_quads.json`, on a pale cloth tinted by the dye) and the blaze (the Halloween Bonfire's flame);
  - the garlands (`textures/entity/pumpkin_vine_garland` and `autumn_leaf_garland`, 64 × 16 in quarters, as the bat bunting's);
  - the stand's goods (the items themselves) and chalk (text).
- **Sounds**: nine original `.ogg` files under `sounds/choir` and `sounds/effigy`, synthesised by `tools/choir_sounds.py`. It needs numpy and ffmpeg with libvorbis, as `tools/drone_sounds.py` does; the files are committed, so the game needs neither.
- **Originality**: all original. The look follows the owner's reference pictures, and nothing is traced from them.

## World and event applicability
Placed by players only, apart from the heirlooms' wild patches. They work all year; nothing depends on the Halloween event, and nothing earned is lost when it ends.
