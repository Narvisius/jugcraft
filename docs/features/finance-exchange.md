# The Exchange: bank, markets, land and shares, in Jugs

Status: implemented as a first batch (draft; not yet compiled or play-tested, see Verification)
Proposal issue: none; feature explained in the PR
Owner: palmierisam03 (contributor), AI-assisted with Claude Opus 5.5
Target milestone and tier: available from the first day; no tech tier
Primary specialty and supported player role: trade and infrastructure. It serves every specialty that sells to another (the "trade" in DESIGN.md), and the player who runs a server as its central bank.

## Player experience

Press **semicolon** (rebindable under Controls > Jugcraft) or type `/jugcraft exchange` to open the **Jugcraft Exchange**, a walnut-and-parchment counting-house ledger with seven tabs. It always opens on the first.

- **Banking:** cash in hand, what you owe the central bank, net worth, how much more you could borrow, and your recent ledger entries.
- **Economy:** the central bank's daily lending rate and per-person limit; a slider to borrow and a box to repay; money held by players, the central reserve, total money, and the ten players holding the most cash. Operators see a **Server Settings** page (the Admin button) that lists every Exchange number with its value and a box to change it.
- **Contracts:** pay another player once, daily or weekly, with a line saying what you get in return ("player 1 pays $100, player 2 promises an electricity connection"). The first payment goes at signing; repeat payments follow every Minecraft day or week until either side ends the contract or the payer cannot pay.
- **Commodities (batch 2):** hold an item and list it at a fixed price per item or as an auction (opening bid, 1, 3 or 7 days). Listed items are held by the Exchange, so sales go through while the seller is offline. Buy part of a fixed lot; bid on auctions (the bid is set aside, an outbid player is refunded at once, the top bidder wins at the close). Items owed to an offline player, or that do not fit, wait in a collection box.
- **Requests (batch 2):** search for any item and post a buy request (the price × quantity is set aside until filled) or a free advertisement. Anyone holding the item can fill a paid request from their inventory and is paid at once.
- **Guide (9 October 2026, contributor: "the guide book should only be for this exchange mod"):** its own book, the **Exchange Guide** item (`finance/ExchangeGuide`, icon map `tools/item_icons/exchange_guide.txt`), given once to every player on first join (tag `jugcraft.exchange_guide`) and again by `/jugcraft exchange guide`; no recipe. `client/ExchangeGuideScreen` shows it in the Exchange's walnut-and-brass style, pages from `assets/jugcraft/exchange_guide/en_us.json` (`tools/exchange_guide.py`). The Exchange's Guide button opens it and returns to the Exchange. The Engineer's Handbook is unchanged.
- **Land (batch 4):** a 15x15 chunk map of the Overworld around the player, coloured from the terrain the client already has loaded (no chunk loading) with claims over it. Buy an unclaimed chunk from the land office at the base price (default 25 Jugs) plus 10% for each chunk already owned (paid to the reserve); list your chunk at an asking price for others to buy (paid to you, so loan repayment applies); give up a chunk (no refund). Claims are protected against breaking, placing, using blocks (doors, chests, machines) and touching entities (animals, frames, armour stands) except by the owner, the owner's trusted players (one list per owner, up to 32) and operators. Admin: `/jugcraft exchange admin land price|unclaim|give`, and a remove button on the map. Saved in `data/jugcraft_land.dat`. The October 21 local [viewer-server update](viewer-server-tools.md) adds four reusable free starter chunks, explicit party sharing plus native explosion/fire protection and fluid/piston ownership boundaries. Indirect modded interactions and external bucket raycasts still need the dedicated abuse audit.
- **Equities (batch 5):** charter a corporation (owner's decisions: an admin-set charter fee, default 250 Jugs, paid to the reserve; treasury plus manual dividends): name, 2-5 letter ticker, up to 1,000,000 shares, a slider (1% steps) or a typed percentage (to 0.01%) for the share offered to the public, and its price. Offered shares become the company's own sell order and their proceeds go to its treasury (a corporate `FinanceBook` account, kept out of the rich list). Shares trade on a per-company limit-order book (buy orders hold money in escrow, sell orders hold shares; crossing orders trade at the resting order's price, best price first, then oldest). The holder of more than half the shares controls the company: pays dividends from the treasury pro rata, changes the logo, and manages company land (chunks zoned in from the Land tab; the controller's trusted players may build). Logos are 16x16 in a 64-colour palette, drawn in an editor or loaded from a PNG (path box, or dropped on the window); uploaded logos are hidden from others until an operator approves them. Overview: price (last trade), last dividend and yield, treasury, an ownership pie chart and holders. Limits: 3 companies per founder, 32 listed, 20 open orders per player. Saved in `data/jugcraft_equities.dat`. Admin: `/jugcraft exchange admin corp fee|approvelogo|removelogo`; admins can cancel any order.

## Connections

- Existing input producer: none in this batch. Money enters only from the central bank (loans and operator grants). Goods enter the economy in the Commodities batch, where every Jugcraft product (metals, parts, fuels, crops) becomes sellable.
- Existing output consumer: none in this batch; markets, land and corporations will spend it.
- Technology connection: planned through the commodities market and the corporation's market chest (factories sell their output). Contracts already let a power producer bill for "an electricity connection" made with the existing cable network.
- Magic connection: none required; magic goods trade like any other once markets open.
- Reachable entry path: nothing to craft or unlock. Every player has an account from their first join.
- Which connections are required vs optional; trade and solo routes: entirely optional. A solo player can ignore it; nothing in progression needs money.
- How this specialty stays useful without mastering every other branch: it is the common medium for trading between specialties, so a farmer and an engineer can exchange without bartering item for item.
- Infrastructure note: this is one shared currency for the whole mod (DESIGN.md: no currency per contributor or school). It is not an item and cannot be crafted, smelted or converted from energy or essence.

## Balance and automation

- **Currency: Jugs.** The Exchange adds no currency of its own (CLAUDE.md, DESIGN.md): every balance it shows, lends, holds or pays is the town's Jugs (`town/Jugs`, owner-approved in [walled-town.md](walled-town.md)), in whole Jugs, through `FinanceBook.Wallet` (`JugsWallet` in game). `Jugs.Data.balances()` was added as a read-only view for the money supply and rich list. Jugs' own maximum balance applies; anything that would pass it goes back to the reserve, so nothing is lost. Default amounts are scaled to the town's prices (the General Store pays about a Jug per iron ingot): starter loan 100, chunks from 25, charter 250.
- **Supply:** the central reserve starts at 1,000,000 Jugs. Money leaves it only as loans or operator grants, and returns as loan repayment. Operators can print into or retire from the reserve with `/jugcraft exchange admin print|retire <amount>`.
- **Loans (reworked 6 October 2026, owner's design):** loans exist to get new players trading. Each player may take one **starter loan** (the base credit limit, default 100 Jugs) from the Banking tab; a join message offers it. Every loan carries a **flat fee** (default 10%, no compounding) and is **due** a set number of days after the first open loan (default 10; borrowing more keeps the due day). While a player owes, a **share of their market earnings** (sales, won auctions, filled requests; default 25%) repays the loan automatically. **Overdue:** a one-time late fee (10% of what is owed), the share doubles, and the player can sell but not buy, bid, post paid requests, pay players, sign contracts or borrow; their repeating contracts lapse. Nothing is taken from inventories. **Credit:** paying off on time (no late fee) multiplies the credit limit by 3, up to three times (27x the base). The limit caps the borrowed part (principal), not the fee. Admins: `/jugcraft exchange admin fee|limit|term|salesshare|debts|forgive|resetstarter`. Old saves: the daily `rate_bp` is no longer read; an open debt from before gets a due day on the next day's close.
- **Conservation:** paying, contracts, borrowing and repaying never create or destroy money (tested: total money is unchanged). Only `print` adds money and only `retire` removes it.
- **Days** are 24,000 ticks of world game time. Sleeping and `/time set` do not skip days. When the server was off, at most 7 missed days are run. Operators can close a day early with `/jugcraft exchange admin nextday` (for events or testing); the shift is saved.
- **Caps:** one amount at most 1,000,000,000 Jugs (Jugs' own maximum balance); debt stops growing at 10^18. Contract notes are 100 characters.
- **Escrow:** bids and paid requests move money from the player's balance into the ledger's escrow; it is paid out to the seller (or filler) or returned, never created. Total money on the Economy tab is cash + escrow + reserve + goods listed at their price (fixed) or standing bid (auction).
- **Inflation:** at each day's close, the average unit price of every item that traded (sales, won auctions, filled requests) is compared with its last traded price; the figure shown is the mean change across those items. It shows "no trades yet" until an item has traded on two different days.
- **Market limits:** 20 listings and 10 requests per player; up to 2,304 items (36 stacks) per lot; a new bid must beat the last by 5% and at least 1 Jug; market data is the overworld's `data/jugcraft_market.dat`.

## Multiplayer and persistence

- **Server authority:** the screen only fills in forms. Every request is re-checked on the server: account, amount range, funds, limit, reserve, contract ownership and operator permission (`Permissions.COMMANDS_GAMEMASTER`) for central bank actions. Formatting codes and control characters are stripped from notes and names.
- **Rate limits:** one action per player every 4 ticks and one refresh per second; extra requests are dropped. The open screen refreshes every 5 seconds and nothing is sent to players who do not have it open.
- **Persistence:** one `SavedData` on the overworld, `data/jugcraft_finance.dat` (`jugcraft:finance`): currency, rate, limit, reserve, last day, accounts (UUID, last name, balance, debt), contracts and the last 200 ledger lines. Missing fields load with defaults. Ended contracts beyond the newest 64 are dropped.
- **Paying offline players:** accounts are found by the player's last name on this server, so you can pay someone who is offline as long as they have joined once.
- **Performance:** the daily check runs every 100 ticks and does work only when a day has passed. No chunk loading, no per-tick per-player work. Market Chests act every 40 ticks and only while loaded.
- **Disable/removal:** the Exchange registers twenty-eight items (the Exchange Guide, the Market Chest, three poker tables, twenty casino games and three scratch cards) and twenty-four blocks with three block entities and a menu; removing the feature removes them from worlds like any mod content. The `.dat` files are simply ignored.
