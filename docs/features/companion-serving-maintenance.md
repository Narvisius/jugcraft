# Companion serving stations, stem fruit and garden maintenance

Owner-requested implementation, 12 October 2026, using OpenAI Codex (GPT-6). Implements the remaining list's items 5–7 (Feast Table/Candy Bowl, Punch Bowl, pumpkin/melon harvesting), followed by optional farmland repair and limited use of available fertilizer. Extends existing Planner assignments, Supply/Output links, Filters, cargo, animations and scheduling. No dependencies or new art assets.

## Serving stations

- **Harvest Feast Table:** assign each table length to restock its two dishes, up to eight servings each. Manually serve the desired foods once. Exact item/component templates remember nonempty dishes, including after they are eaten empty; serving another food changes that dish's template. The existing nine-item Filter limits which remembered foods are replenished. An empty Filter allows every remembered dish. Supply Off stops replenishment. Empty, never-stocked dishes report “Stock table dishes first.” Existing dining buffs and joined-table logic are unchanged. No Output extraction is exposed.
- **Candy Bowl:** Supply restocks native treat-tag items up to the existing 64-treat capacity. Its Filter limits allowed treats; empty allows any native treat. The companion must satisfy the bowl owner's existing food-access policy. Visitors' nightly limits, trick-or-treaters and owner access are unchanged. No Output extraction is exposed.
- **Witch's Brew Punch Bowl:** Supply brings ingredients in the native berry tag and empty glass bottles. Each berry adds two servings, up to twelve; one real bottle and one serving produce one native bottled punch. A new unticked block entity stores up to twelve completed bottles for Output, separate from visible unbottled punch. It persists and drops real bottles on removal; empty-hand sneak-use retrieves buffered bottles manually. Restocking stops when the buffer is full. No processing-speed bonus or additional per-tick task is introduced. Existing player berry/bottle interactions still work.

The shared serving adapter participates in Fabric transactions. Stock, templates, bottles and pending punch changes roll back with failed/simulated transfers; client fill-state notifications and punch sounds occur only after commit. Multiple companions recheck actual capacity on delivery. Cargo remains physical and uses normal load-scaled hauling effort. The planner's bounded source/destination search is reused; there is no station or player scan. Ordinary loaded-position and town-protection checks apply. Serving Filters are saved on their station and shared by assigned companions.

## Pumpkin and melon stems

Existing Farmland zones recognize vanilla pumpkin/melon stems and their fruit. A fruit is harvested only when a matching attached stem points at it, and that stem is accessible inside the same assignment. This avoids arbitrary pumpkin-block removal and cross-boundary stem changes. The fruit's native block drops go to marked cargo/Output, with stems left planted and normal neighbor updates allowing them to produce again. Full cargo leaves the fruit unchanged.

The planting selector accepts pumpkin/melon seeds, with pumpkin or melon icons normalized to their seed. Real seeds are supplied and planted into remembered stem spots, or valid empty farmland when explicitly selecting that crop. Leave dirt/empty fruit spaces beside the stems. Stems are not automatically removed when switching the crop selection. Fruit picking uses the existing general interaction at 4 JE/t for 80 ticks (320 JE), without hoe wear or a speed bonus; planting retains ordinary garden timing. No growth or loot multiplier is added.

## Garden maintenance

The Farmland job's gear opens two independent options, both **Off** in new and migrated saves: **Repair farmland** and **Fertilizing** (Auto/Off). The seed ghost slot remains beside the gear. Options follow job reordering, compaction and save/reload, without changing the assignment/transport identity. Packet edits retain menu, owner/party, distance, anchor and protection validation. Orchard and legacy non-zone plots do not expose these controls.

Repair learns up to 256 actual vanilla/rich farmland positions per zone during bounded discovery. Only a learned position which has reverted to its matching dirt/rich soil with air above may be tilled again; paths, arbitrary dirt, planters and Verdant Beds are not converted. Replacing remembered soil with another block forgets it when observed. A hoe is required, takes one durability and uses the existing downward harvest clip for 40 ticks at 4 JE/t (160 JE). Farmland which was already dirt before Peepo observed it must first be tilled by the player. Repairs do not create water or guarantee hydration.

Auto fertilizing uses available **bone meal, bat guano and Fertilizer** (`jugcraft:fertilizer`, chemical superphosphate), fetching at most **four total fertilizer items** through linked Supply across this companion's gardens. Manually given stacks are not truncated. Bone meal targets one eligible crop; guano uses its native 3×3 reach and one dose; superphosphate uses its native 5×5 reach and two doses. Area fertilizers retain their crop-tag restriction and one-block vertical reach. Ordinary crop, tall-crop, cranberry, sweet-berry and cocoa growth uses the native bone-meal behavior. Magic crops, trees, grass, stems and unsupported blocks are excluded. Area applications skip cells outside the zone, protected/unloaded cells and crops still on cooldown. Tall crops validate their possible upper sections before growth.

Harvesting/replanting is scanned before maintenance within the existing rolling discovery window. One maintenance application is allowed per companion/zone every **20 seconds**; successfully fertilized crop positions have a **60-second shared cooldown** across companions and overlapping zones. One fertilizer is spent only if a native application succeeds. Fertilizing takes 80 ticks at 4 JE/t (320 JE) and uses the existing interaction clip, without hoe wear. Failed growth attempts back off. The shared runtime crop cooldown table is bounded to 4,096 positions per loaded level with incremental expiry cleanup; when full, applications back off instead of evicting active protections. Zone timers and learned soil persist on the companion; shared crop timers are transient across a server restart.

Discovery retains the 32-cell slice/window and four-approach cap under shared server admission budgets; maintenance adds a second pass over the same bounded cells. Fertilizer completion examines at most 75 cells and two native doses, with no chunk loading or fake player. Native mob-griefing, energy/rest, inventory reservations and per-cell worker leases still apply. No irrigation, magical habitat maintenance, trellis construction or support repair is added.
