Status: merged to main ([#295](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/pull/295), integrated by
[#277](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/pull/277)); see Verification for what has run.
Proposal issue: none; the owner asked for the magic content to continue on 9 October 2026 and chose to build the
elemental schools with their own supplied magic art. This part needs none of that art; part 2 brings it in.
Owner: @jimbozoomer-byte. AI-assisted implementation with Claude Code; the model is named in the commit trailers.
Target milestone and tier: Practitioner stage (Jugcraft Workshops), beside the Alembic Arts. Ember is the first of the
four elemental Principles (Ember, Rime, Tempest, Strata) the Concordance named on 5 October and left unbuilt.
Primary specialty and supported player role: the Hearthbinders ("controlled heat for kilns, forges and cooking").
Combat (a fire bolt and a burst), defense (Fire Resistance) and utility (lighting hearths at a distance).

## Player experience

- **Hearthbinding** is a new research entry. Once First Light is understood, **sneak and use** something that holds
  fire to examine it: coal, charcoal, a torch or a campfire from the start, and magma, blaze powder, blaze rods, magma
  cream and fire charges later. Three different ones observe it; studying one at a **Lampwright's Bench**, or reading
  someone's Research Notes, understands it.
- Understood, it teaches three **invocations**, cast with any Concordance instrument like First Light's:
  - **Hearthspark** (utility, 3 Focus): kindles the unlit campfire, candle or candle cake you look at, up to 16 blocks
    away, as flint and steel would. It never places fire.
  - **Hearthguard** (defense, 5 Focus): Fire Resistance for 30 seconds (tuned with Prolong, 60).
  - **Cinderbolt** (damage, 5 Focus): a burning shard, 3 fire damage to the first creature on its line (+0.5 per point
    of fire Spell Power), which then **smoulders** for 3 seconds.
- **Mastery** is practice: kindle **three different kinds of hearth** with Hearthspark (campfire, soul campfire, candle,
  candle cake; a second campfire counts for nothing more). Mastered, it teaches **Hearthflare** (damage, 6 Focus): 4
  fire damage and Smoulder to the four nearest creatures within 3 blocks of you, never you. Mastery also lowers each
  Ember invocation's cost by one.
- **Smoulder** is a new status: while it lasts the creature burns as anything on fire does (vanilla's damage, a point a
  second). Water and rain put it out and end it; Fire Resistance and fire-born creatures ignore it; milk cures it. It
  never sets blocks alight.
- A new **Hearth** codex category explains Hearthbinding and Smoulder; each invocation has its page in Invocations,
  which opens when it is learned. The journal shows Hearthbinding's progress like any entry's.

## How it works

- Data (generated by `tools/concordance_ember.py`, merged by `tools/concordance.py`):
  `data/jugcraft/concordance/research/hearthbinding.json`, four invocations and five authored components
  (`kindling`, `hearthguard_ward`, `cinder`, `smoulder`, `flare`), four Spell Engine spells in the fire school, the
  practice gate `concordance/practice/hearthkeeping.json`, the item tag `#jugcraft:ember_specimens` (also in
  `#jugcraft:concordance_specimens`), advancements and codex pages.
- The invocations are compositions in the shared grammar, compiled under the instrument's limits like First Light's:
  `ray struck kindling`, `here struck hearthguard_ward`, `ray struck cinder smoulder`, `here creatures flare smoulder`.
- Java (`concordance/ember`): **one change to the shared effects**: an alteration whose school is Ember's
  (`spell_power:fire`) kindles a hearth (`CampfireBlock`/`CandleBlock`/`CandleCakeBlock.canLight`, as flint and steel
  decides) instead of putting out fire (`Ember.kindles`, `Ember.kindle`; `ConcordanceEffects.accepts` and the spread
  selection now take the effect, not only its kind). `Ember.kindle` records the `jugcraft:hearthkeeping` practice for
  the player behind it, keyed by the kind of hearth, once they understand Hearthbinding (the practice's gate).
- **Smoulder** (`SmoulderEffect`) is a vanilla status effect: whenever the creature's fire has burnt out it relights it
  for a second, so it never burns faster than vanilla fire; in water or rain, or on a fire-immune creature, it ends
  itself.
- Examining a fire specimen says it holds fire (`message.jugcraft.concordance.examine.heat`).

## Connections

- Input producer: First Light (Hearthbinding needs it understood); coal, charcoal, torches and campfires from the
  Overworld; Nether fire items for players who go there.
- Output consumer: players and creatures through the shared effect boundary; vanilla campfires, candles and candle
  cakes (lit campfires cook food and signal with smoke).
- Technology connection: lighting the hearths the kitchen and smokehouse use; fire Spell Power from any equipment that
  grants it (part 2's regalia does: [record](arcane-concordance-ember-regalia.md)).
- Magic connection: the first non-Radiance invocations; the same research, notes, practice, stage and Conclave rules
  as every entry (the Conclave now counts the Hearthbinders as one of its traditions); fire Spell Power scales Cinderbolt
  and Hearthflare like arcane Spell Power scales the Lance.
- Reachable entry path: First Light understood → Hearthbinding by examining coal, charcoal and a torch → the bench →
  mastery with a campfire, a candle and a candle cake. Nothing needs the Nether or the End (`check_mod_data` runs the
  progression graph without them).
- Solo, trade and cooperative routes: entirely solo; Research Notes can be shared.
- Specialty use without other branches: needs only First Light.

## Balance

- Each invocation costs at least what its composition costs a composer (the loader and `check_mod_data` enforce it),
  and its cooldown is never shorter than the composition's.
- **Cinderbolt is a little below the Lance up front** (3 against 5) and earlier (understood, not mastered); its
  Smoulder adds about 3 over its 3 seconds, which water, rain, Fire Resistance and fire immunity answer. Hearthflare (4
  damage to at most four creatures, 10 s cooldown, 6 Focus) is the mastered area spell.
- **Hearthguard** gives 30 seconds of Fire Resistance on a 20-second cooldown for 5 Focus. A vanilla potion gives 3 or
  8 minutes; Hearthguard's advantage is that it needs no Nether ingredients, its cost is Focus a player regains (one
  point every 2 seconds).
- Hearthspark makes nothing a system consumes: no fuel, no heat a machine reads, so there is no conversion loop. Lighting
  a campfire saves a flint and steel's wear or a fire charge.
- Smoulder is vanilla burning: a point of damage a second, no attribution (like any fire), no spreading.

## Multiplayer and persistence

- Server authority: every cast runs on the server through the invocation path (instrument, research, Focus and rate
  checks). Kindling asks `Authority.mayChange` for the person behind it (claims, protected towns, spawn protection; a
  caster who is away waits); harm asks the PvP rules and parties and `Authority.mayStrike`, as for every harmful effect.
- Persistence: research and practice in the player's Concordance knowledge (already versioned, step 30); Smoulder is a
  vanilla status effect and saves with the creature; a lit campfire or candle is a vanilla block state. No new saved
  data.
- Disable behaviour: with `concordance.enabled=false`, examining and casting are refused like every Concordance action;
  everything stays registered.

## Dependencies and assets

No new dependency. Framework use: **Spell Engine** (four new spells in the fire school), **Spell Power** (fire Spell
Power scales the two damage invocations), **Modonomicon** (the Hearth category and four invocation pages).

Original assets, drawn by code: the four spell icons and the Smoulder status icon (`tools/concordance_ember_art.py`,
32x32 on the shared violet ground; 18x18 for the status), and four sounds synthesised by `tools/concordance_sounds.py`
(`ember_gather`, `hearthspark`, `hearthguard`, `cinderbolt`: crackle, breaths of flame and warm low tones in the
Concordance's key). Hearthflare's release was synthesised too; since part 2 it plays the owner's four "pyro" fire
recordings instead, and the drawn cue is gone. Part 2 searched the owner's library for the rest; why the drawn icons and
cues stay is in [its record](arcane-concordance-ember-regalia.md#dependencies-and-assets).
