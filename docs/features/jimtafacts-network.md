# Jimtafacts digital storage and workshops

Local implementation in progress, 23 October 2026. AI assistance: OpenAI Codex (GPT-6).
The owner requested the complete collection and deferred tests until the combined implementation is finished.
**This code has not yet been compiled or tested.** It is not a released pack or a claim of complete AE source parity.

## Implemented locally

- Removable 1k/4k/16k/64k/256k item and fluid cells, ten-cell drives and one-cell chests. Actual contents are carried by the cell's saved component; there is no separate inventory database to leave phantom copies behind. Item cells allow 63 types and eight items per byte; fluid cells allow 63 types and 1,024 buckets per k. These are documented native capacities, not a claim of the source's precise byte-overhead formula.
- Controller, energy acceptor, standard/dense JE cells and a coal/charcoal/coke vibration chamber. Powered blocks retain their JE in their dropped item. Source charge-level and online/offline models are selected from saved state. JE is shared with Jugcraft energy cables and generators.
- Searchable item and fluid terminals; a 3×3 crafting terminal with live vanilla recipe results; linked wireless storage/crafting handsets with a 64-block loaded-dimension range. Catalog icons cannot be picked up as real stacks. The fluid terminal exchanges real containers held on the cursor and reports millibuckets.
- Filtered import/export buses, external Storage Buses and a buffered item/fluid interface. Storage Buses expose the original container contents without copying them into cells; repeated buses on one container or either half of a double chest are de-duplicated. Transfers use native Fabric transactions and recheck authority/claims. Redstone pauses automated work. Cables have connected arms instead of disconnected floating stubs.
- Encoded patterns, pattern providers, queued Crafting Units and repeating Molecular Assemblers. Current server recipes decide output and remainders. Intermediate recipes are resolved from installed patterns with cycle/depth/work limits. A failed recipe rolls back its inputs, intermediate results and JE. Queues and physical per-job ingredient reservations persist; the later scheduling section describes their exact scope.
- Cell Workbench item/fluid partitioning. Real sample items or filled buckets remain in the workbench. Filters reject new unwanted inputs but never delete or trap existing cell contents. The I/O Port fills or empties finite item/fluid cells against real connected storage and returns completed cells.
- Certus Charger and Inscriber with reusable presses, real inputs and blocked-output handling. Existing circuit assemblers still make the same processors. Charged certus improves fluix yield, giving the charger a useful optional route.
- Eleven original quartz/fluix/sky-stone families, each with a block, stairs, slabs and walls. Four budding-quartz grades and four growth stages provide renewable certus; lower grades can degrade and be restored. The flawless grade is renewable without degradation. Existing worlds are not regenerated.
- Original drive bays, cell status LEDs, inscriber press geometry/easing and charger bob use sanitized render data. Original quartz fixtures, connected glass, powered growth accelerators, source tool/equipment family and memory-card visuals are included; see the equipment and rendering records.
- All 85 supplied glass/covered/smart/dense cable colors now have their source meshes and atlases. Different colors separate direct cable connections; transparent fluix joins colors. Native deterministic loaded routes allocate eight channels on ordinary cables, 32 on dense cables and eight devices without a controller. Smart indicators show segment usage (four channels per dense bar), rather than the source multipart per-face/channel solver.
- Machine processing patterns have a no-typing 9-input/3-output editor. Providers dispatch real ingredients to a facing inventory and wait for actual outputs; a separate import bus can acknowledge returns from another port of the same machine. A declared recipe never creates outputs. Crafting monitors show bounded queues and validate exact cancellation targets. Already delivered ingredients stay in the machine on cancellation.
- Portable item and fluid cells use the same searchable terminal. Their contents and JE remain in the held item, rollback together and become inaccessible through the old screen as soon as that item is moved. They recharge in the existing JE system/charger.
- Item/fluid external storage and dedicated fluid import/export buses use real adjacent inventories/tanks, exact filters and rollback. Level and energy emitters have a sample selector, threshold buttons, polarity and actual stock readings.
- Reusable silicon/processor dies print original intermediate components before joining them into processors. Existing four-input and circuit-assembler routes remain valid. Cell housings, cores, dusts, fluix pearls, wireless receivers and boosters have acquisition recipes and uses.
- Matter Condenser consumes real items or fluid at the supplied ratios: 256 units per matter ball and 256,000 per singularity, with component-sized credit capacity. Default behavior never silently voids overflow; output blockage stops production. Stored credit survives save/reload and block-item pickup. Source trash mode is intentionally absent from this native port.
- Wireless Access Points extend the linked handset's coverage from each loaded connected point: 64 blocks plus two per booster, up to 192. They do not load chunks or cross dimensions. Redstone disables the point.
- Formation planes place filtered simple blocks or eject actual items; annihilation planes collect nearby unreserved drops and harvest the facing non-container block with real loot. Both are claim-aware, power-limited and operate on one loaded position per second. Multi-block/container placement and container breaking are deliberately refused.

## Preservation and security

`tools/jimtafacts_network.py` and `tools/jimtafacts_network_decor.py` resolve the supplied model dependency graph. Model coordinates, rotations, UVs, source credits and texture pixels are retained. Namespace references and source-loader light/tint metadata are adapted for Fabric. Texture animation sidecars are copied unchanged. `network-runtime-copies.json` records each runtime asset's source; no reference executable or third-party JAR is shipped.

Network discovery is bounded to 256 loaded blocks and rechecks owner/party and land permissions. An oversized or interrupted network fails closed. Handsets do not load chunks or cross dimensions. Item/fluid transfers, crafting inputs/results/remainders and JE use one transaction. Cells cannot contain other digital cells. Passive fluid ports use the existing claim-aware fluid transport system. Crafting is limited to 64 recipe steps and 16 dependency levels per attempt, with at most 16 queued orders of 64 crafts per Crafting Unit. Processing jobs wait for machine results and never infer completion merely from elapsed time. Source code and original guide/data were inspected statically, never executed.

- Saved facade covers and anchors on each cable face; original anchor/frame meshes. Holding the Network Tool reveals cables. Its nine-slot wallet holds real upgrade cards and locks the held tool while open.

- Original meteorite generation, compass mesh and Tiny TNT; source sky chest/tank geometry and actual capacities, crank and light detector behavior. Source debug/internal items are tracked separately.
- 2³ and 16³ spatial cells, original cell/port art and recovered pylon cube/overlay geometry. Eight corner pylons select a bounded static building box. The port exchanges it with a server-side plot ledger, charging 1,000 + 50 JE per block. A write-ahead journal precedes any change; copied stale cell revisions cannot retrieve a plot twice. Up to 64 vanilla barrels, hoppers and idle dispensers/droppers retain their contents, names, locks and unopened loot through the same journal. Container type markers prevent cross-type payloads; old barrel-only payloads remain readable. Pending or powered dispenser/dropper pulses are rejected. Open menus and stale inventory handles close before detachment, and outgoing block callbacks are cleared before restoring contents and notifying neighbors. Entities, remaining containers, fluids, active machines and unsupported materials are refused. This does not claim full source dimension/entity migration.
- The original guide item opens a searchable native encyclopedia page explaining these systems.
- Incremental dependencies span crafting and asynchronous machine patterns. A failed atomic craft can prepare one intermediate or queue one real machine batch, then retries only against actual storage contents. Planning is bounded to 128 missing-ingredient visits and 16 levels; existing pending output prevents repeat enqueueing. Machine jobs request their own intermediates. Craft pattern matching includes recipe mode and identity, not just the ingredient grid. The jobs screen distinguishes dependency work, missing base ingredients and waiting for machine output.

## Local scheduling continuation

Implemented with OpenAI Codex, user-selected Astra High. **Not compiled or tested.**

- A prerequisite can now go ahead of its undelivered parent on the same serial provider. A batch already delivered to a physical machine cannot be displaced. Queues still cap at 16 processing jobs per provider and the CPU's tier limit.
- Each job tree has a saved UUID. Its direct batch ingredients are withdrawn into physical escrow, up to 576 items per participating node/root and 64 roots per node. Other terminal users and jobs cannot withdraw that escrow. Participants share the root's reserved inventory instead of reserving the same requirement twice. No expected machine output is materialized. Reservations and consumption participate in ordinary Fabric rollback.
- Reservation withdrawal, later consumption and return use normal JE transfer costs. On cancellation, unused reserved items return to available storage; items that cannot fit or lack transfer power drop at their holding node. Breaking a node drops its actual escrow and cancels associated work.
- Cancellation stops queued descendants. Already-paid machine batches retain exactly one completion record and return their real output to storage; ingredients are not refunded twice. A saved server ledger remembers disconnected/unloaded participants until they acknowledge cancellation before further work. It never loads their chunks. It caps at 4,096 active roots and 256 participant positions per root; reaching a bound pauses/rejects additional work explicitly.
- Reservations concern the currently loaded local network, not a global stock lock across quantum networks. Disconnecting a node can release its unused escrow if its root is no longer locally active. Materials remain real and conserved, but reconnecting may require reserving them again.
- The original Creative Storage Cell model is now used. Creative players configure one item type or one fluid using an other-hand sample, and sneak-use clears it. There is no survival recipe or survival configuration. It supplies that template indefinitely through ordinary JE-paid extraction and rejects deposits. Container-like items and nested cells cannot be configured as item sources. This is a single-template native adaptation.

Authored, not run: escrow privacy/rollback/return, shared reservations, disconnected cancellation acknowledgement, paid-batch cancellation, same-provider prerequisite ordering and creative-source restrictions in `DigitalJobGameTests`.

## Cable attachments, cell handling and anchors — local continuation

OpenAI Codex, user-selected Astra High. Implementation and resource generation only; **not compiled or tested**.

- Thirty-two attachment items can share the six faces of an ME cable: six item/fluid buses; five terminals; storage/conversion and three decorative monitors; compact interface, energy acceptor and pattern provider; two planes; two emitters; all six P2P tunnels; quartz fiber and both toggle buses. Each face owns its real slots, filter, identity and saved work. Existing standalone blocks remain valid. A face occupied by a cover, anchor or cable connection cannot accept an attachment. Visible cable arms reach the attachments; outward machine ports do not accidentally bridge ME networks.
- Sneak-wrench removes only the selected attachment, returns its real contents/charge and invalidates old menus and cached transfer ports. Owners/party permission protect changes and whole-cable removal. Breaking the host drops all attached parts and their actual contents. Per-face provider job-ledger entries keep cancellation acknowledgements separate; old block-only ledger saves migrate as standalone entries.
- Mounted terminals and monitors retain the supplied meshes, front screens and on/off art. Monitor public item/count overlays rotate onto all six faces. Render packets carry appearance only, not private inventories, filters, owners or job state. P2P typed models inherit their base exactly once, preventing coincident duplicate faces. Planes now use the supplied one-pixel face panel, all 16 adjacent-edge permutations and status indicators instead of the centered inventory mesh. Matching loaded neighboring planes join their edges once per second.
- Mounted emitters drive only their outward face. P2P memory cards resolve the saved node identity inside a host, so swapping/removing an attachment invalidates the old route. Mounted light tunnels use an on/off, full-brightness host lamp to avoid multiplying every colored cable's state count by 16; standalone light tunnels retain analog brightness. Toggle attachments gate their outward network route from actual redstone; quartz-fiber attachments share bounded JE between the host and facing network without joining their item/channel networks. The connectors are passive, while actual device attachments count individually against channels, including on a controller-free root segment.
- I/O Port has **Fill cell / Empty cell** controls. Transfers cost ordinary JE, move at most 64 items or one bucket per second, use transactions and pause if completion output is occupied. Input is automated from above, completed output from other faces. Full/empty completion is explicit; a fill job with insufficient matching stock waits rather than declaring the cell finished. Creative templates are refused. Fluid partitions permit existing stored fluid to be withdrawn even after filters change.
- Spatial Anchors renew expiring nonpersistent tickets for their current chunk, cost 100 JE/second and cap at four per owner / 32 per server. They obey the existing absent-owner policy. Lost authority/power, redstone, removal or expired renewal releases the lease. Multiple anchors in one chunk share ticket lifetime correctly. After restart, visiting the loaded workshop reactivates it. This is not arbitrary remote chunk selection or persistent forced-chunk restoration.

Authored for the final batch, **not run**: separate attachment saves/filters, render-data privacy, per-face provider cancellation, detached-menu/energy-port invalidation, exact charge recovery, root-segment channel accounting, mounted emitter/lamp behavior, toggle routing, joined plane edges, finite-cell I/O rollback, fluid partition behavior and paid anchor shutdown.
