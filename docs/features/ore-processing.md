# Ore processing depth

Status: implemented in source (PR #16); **not yet played**. Compiles in CI; seven game tests cover it.
Proposal issue: none; the owner selected "Processing depth" directly on 30 September 2026.
Owner: @jimbozoomer-byte
Target milestone and tier: Workshops (technology). The pulverizer, sieve and sawmill are early; the ore washer comes after invar and circuits.
Primary specialty and supported player role: mining and processing

## Player experience
More metal per ore for more investment, plus useful extras:

| Route | Ingots per ore |
| --- | --- |
| furnace | 1 |
| crusher or **pulverizer** → furnace | 2 (the pulverizer also rolls a byproduct) |
| **ore washer** → pulverizer → furnace | 3 |

The **sieve** finds flint and small amounts of metal in gravel, and quartz and gold in soul sand. The **sawmill** gets more planks per log, plus sawdust (4 → paper).

Details and numbers: [TECH_TREE.md → Ore processing](../TECH_TREE.md#ore-processing).

## Connections
- **Existing input producers:** every Jugcraft and vanilla ore (copper, iron, gold); water from springs, pumps and pipes; gravel from the crusher.
- **Existing output consumers:**
  - Dusts smelt in the furnace, the electric furnace or the arc furnace.
  - Byproduct metals feed alloys and parts.
  - Planks and sticks feed vanilla crafting.
- **Technology connection:** the washer uses the fluid system. Everything automates with [item logistics](item-logistics.md), including the byproduct slots.
- **Magic connection:** none yet.
- **Reachable entry path:**
  - The pulverizer needs iron gears and bronze plates (metal press).
  - The ore washer needs invar plates, bronze gears and a basic circuit, all made earlier.
  - Nothing here gates those.
- **Required vs optional:** optional. The crusher and plain smelting remain.

## Balance and automation
- **Energy per operation:**
  - Pulverizer ore: 4,000 JE (20 JE/t for 200 ticks).
  - Ore washer: 3,200 JE plus 500 mB water.
  - Sieve: 800 JE.
  - Sawmill: 1,200 JE.
- **Metal is audited by `tools/check_mod_data.py`:**
  - Only ore blocks gain, ×2 or ×3.
  - Washed ore, raw metal and ingots grind 1:1.
  - Byproducts add at most 25% of the input's metal on average (10%, or 5% for tin → tungsten).
  - Sieve finds (from gravel, no metal in) average 0.2 nugget per operation, below the one-nugget cap.
- **No conversion loops:** ingot → dust → ingot is 1:1, and nothing turns dust back into ore.
- **Byproduct safety:** a machine only finishes when every possible byproduct has room, so none is voided.
  - A byproduct from a disabled feature is never made.

## Multiplayer and persistence
- Server-authoritative; byproducts are rolled with the server level's random source.
- Machines save items, energy, progress and the washer's water (`"water"`).
- New IDs only; no migrations.
- Disable behavior: the recipes need the `machines` switch plus the metal's own switch. Items stay registered.
