> Owner direction, 21 October 2026: Treat the current Frostward College as the **skeleton**, add architectural strength and individualized detail as the **muscle and skin**, and keep **enchanted autumn / Halloween** as its unmistakable identity. This is an implementation specification for draft [PR #448](https://github.com/jimbozoomer-byte/jugcraft/pull/448), not a replacement college.

## Selective world-to-Frostward integration workflow

This is **part reuse and remodeling**, not just viewing reference images:

1. **Catalog the supplied Minecraft 1.12.2 world:** inspect region data and block types; identify specific strong examples such as the courtyard wall/bays, building transitions, facade relief, stair cores, vaulted halls, library modules, tower cornices, furnished chambers and bridge stonework. Compare each with its destination in the *current* 26.3 Frostward source.
2. **Choose donors surgically:** select bounded architectural sections or interior features, not whole campus chunks or the entire prebuilt College. Rank by how much structural/detail improvement they bring. Log source area, target room, approximate adaptation, and whether it is geometry, materials or furnishing.
3. **Adapt rather than paste unchanged:** resize and reshape selected sections for the 176 × 76 × 161 or subsequently expanded Frostward layout. Convert obsolete 1.12.2 block IDs/metadata to 26.3 block states; preserve valid block-entity semantics, verify collision, block support, light, stairs, doors and palette registrations. Rework roofs, supports, entrances and transitions to fit existing rooms and stations. Avoid large-scale loss of existing game logic or puzzle anchors.
4. **Regenerate through the single authoritative generator:** reflect every accepted adaptation in `tools/winterhold_layout.json`, `tools/winterhold_layout.py`, `tools/winterhold_academy.py`, or existing furnishing modules; do not paste over the generated NBT and leave source out of sync. A documented intermediate extraction or converter script may be used for selected pieces; it must not make the donor map a required runtime dependency.
5. **Make the autumn conversion explicit:** remove or replace source snow layers/white covering, snowy landscaping and unintended ice, while preserving selected useful Nordic stone geometry. Replace exterior look with Jugcraft autumn foliage, leaf piles, jack-o'-lanterns and ambient effects, keeping warm lanternlight and sinister mist.
6. **Verify each integrated section:** show before/after placement views, actual runtime bridge and top-down silhouettes, indoor captures with lighting, connected routes, 45-point room ledger and workstation/NPC/puzzle checks. Record adapted source sections and credit. Do not mark a donor piece accepted just because its blocks fit.

## Material and detail density

Avoid a wall-to-wall mono-material `stone_bricks` look and long featureless corridors. Integrate permitted Jugcraft block variants and existing player-owned architectural/decor sets after verifying registry names and authored asset provenance. Stone should visibly bear weight, arches should spring from columns, roof projections should have supports, decorative features should not obstruct doors, pathing or contraptions.

Define each flagship room in multiple zones: **structural form**, **useful fixtures**, **furnishing clusters**, **lighting**, **lived-in clutter**, **secret or point of interest**. At least one intentional visual focal point and one credible purpose per room. Avoid adding 100 shelves to a tiny space and calling it a grand library.

## Halloween atmosphere is mandatory and takes priority over the reference's snow

Fonta59's build depicts the snowy Skyrim College; **do not bring snow over**. Continue PR #448's permanent local enchanted-autumn identity. No snowcaps, snow particles, snowy conifers or icy-blue grading as the default campus treatment. Preserve gray Nordic stone and replace environmental appearance with red/orange/gold leaves, autumn foliage, deliberate pumpkin clusters, warm candle/lantern illumination, local tumbling leaf particles and restrained creepy ground fog. Keep pale teal or icy runes only for specifically magical/forbidden rooms.

This aesthetic must persist through all server seasons without changing neighboring biome or actual world weather. Reuse existing Jugcraft leaf piles, pumpkin carvings, string lights, garlands, autumn trees and FogParticle where appropriate. Respect `academyfx off/low/normal`, particle settings, bounded client performance, indoor suppression and cleanup on world/dimension exit. Don't let ambiance hide navigational landmarks or make the campus into a Halloween carnival.
