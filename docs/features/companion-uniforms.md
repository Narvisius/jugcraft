# Companion job uniforms

Owner-requested implementation, 24 October 2026, with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6).

## Outcome and progression

Twelve nonstackable craftable uniform items use the existing costume slot for both Peepo and Jughead. The original pumpkin remains valid. The slot and quick-move paths accept the uniforms; direct interaction fills only an empty costume slot, never deletes an existing outfit. Creative entries, recipe-book unlocks, names, item models and bonus tooltips are included. Recipes consume three colored wool, two string, one leather and a job-specific ingredient (see the twelve recipes in the player guide). Ordinary farming, husbandry, fishing and early metalworking supply these items; no new station or dependency is required.

Farmer: crop actions run at 1.15x speed and consume 15% less reserve per working tick. A successful harvest has a 15% chance to add one produce item. Apply this only within actual crop harvesting, after the replant reserve, not generic marked-cargo insertion. Standard crops, supported additional crops, planter crops, mushroom colonies and native magic crop yields are covered. Orchards, herb drying and maintenance are excluded. For crops with a separate seed, bonus selection prefers produce; carrots/potatoes can also be their own planting item.

Fisherman: bite waiting is reduced by 15% with a 20-tick floor, fishing effort by 20%, and each landed catch containing a fish has a 15% chance to duplicate its first fish stack. `minecraft:fishes` supplies compatibility with tagged modded fish. Native treasure/junk chances and rod durability are unchanged. A duplicate enters the existing saved pending catch/cargo path, not a separate pickup entity.

Chef: cutting-board and candy-pulling action durations are divided by 1.2 and rounded up. Productive pot/cutting/candy energy and kitchen Supply/Output travel energy are discounted 10%. Pot/skillet/stove/board/candy/canning routes are identified by their registered station IDs; general porter routes do not inherit a kitchen bonus from their destination. Native cooking/heating/cooling and assistance progress are unchanged. Transfers that were instantaneous remain so, avoiding an artificial slowdown to give a handling bonus meaning.

Mechanic: shared ProcessorJob and StarterWorkshopJob productive debits receive a 25% discount. Machine-based effort, helper counts, quarter-speed contribution, input/fuel requirements and its generator conversion rates are unchanged. No goggles are modeled.

Porter: ordinary supply/output and dedicated porter travel/handling debits receive a 25% discount. A transient 25% movement-speed modifier is present only during an active delivery with matching real carried cargo, outside editing, eating and furniture rest. The modifier is added/removed on state change and does not rewrite base speed or persist as a permanent attribute. Empty legs still receive the energy saving, but not movement speed. The backpack adds no inventory capacity.

## Correctness and server cost

Costume identity is synchronized as one small entity-data value and reconstructed from the actual saved slot, including client-only crate previews. Uniform bonuses are mutually exclusive. Hand tools retain their existing ownership, stowing and fetching rules. Wearing an outfit adds no world searches, recipe scans, inventories, fake players, timers or additional AI goals.

Discounted work uses the existing energy transaction, capacity and per-tick output limit. A persisted fractional remainder supplies accurate savings for small debits. Even zero-cost fractional work ticks mark the companion as working, preventing accidental passive/rest regeneration. Runner discounts the reserve charged for wheel/crank work, while accepted output units and the per-tick output limit stay unchanged. Every other uniform leaves generator extraction undiscounted. Crank simulation and its actual debit use the same transactional rate. Failed transactions restore both reserve and fractional remainder.

A saved deterministic bonus-roll sequence advances only on committed harvest/catch work. A full cargo rollback cannot reroll an extra-yield chance. Crop base and bonus insertion share the existing harvest/world transaction; fish bonuses join the already-persistent catch. No player-provided cargo, supplies, seeds spent on planting, or delivered outputs are multiplied.

## Chef production bonus

Owner follow-up: Chef receives a 15% chance to double the actual output batch, including recipe counts and components, for cooking-pot meals, skillet/stove foods, cutting-board results and completed candy. No extra ingredient, fuel, knife durability or handling charge is added for a bonus. Canning only seals food already produced; that step and returned ingredient containers/trays do not roll again. Other uniforms, native furnace automation and hearth baking are unchanged.

Cutting/candy output preflight and the deterministic bonus sequence share the existing transaction. A full output or failed debit restores the roll and leaves the input/tray available. The cutting job reports Full for insufficient result capacity. Pot rolls require a currently present, permitted Chef with a productive assist within the last tick; collection alone is never enough. A single saved pending recipe result holds a pot bonus that cannot fit yet, blocks further cooking, retries insertion once per second, and is released with normal inventory/break drops.

Skillet/stove raw-food deliveries roll per accepted input inside the real insertion transaction. Skillet stores a bounded 16-bit FIFO mask and the stove stores one flag per hob slot. Snapshots, save/load, normal completion, manual take/add and removed recipes handle those flags. Old saves and ordinary player-loaded food default to no bonus. Reloading, replacing the worker or picking up cooked food cannot reroll the same input. Skillet bonuses join the ordinary fried stack when they fit; overflow uses the kitchen adapter's existing bounded result slots and remain available to Output, manual take-all and block-break drops. A blocked skillet retries completion once per second rather than copying the result buffer every tick.

## Rendering and provenance

The existing native/procedural companion renderer draws lightweight uniform geometry attached to the current head, body, arms and leg poses. Shape data, face normals and material groups are constructed once; rendering does not build or filter an outfit mesh per frame. There are no new animation owners or framework dependencies. The owner's visual follow-up replaces every wool texture reference in worn outfits and inventory models. Smooth dyed cloth uses the cloth-only UV area of Minecraft's native banner surface; item models use native bed cloth with constant item tints. Existing Jugcraft `dz_canvas`, `scarecrow_straw` and `pi_leather` surfaces supply canvas clothing/bags, straw hats/creels and leather belts/straps. Hardware uses native iron. Native textures are referenced at runtime, never copied into the repository. The corresponding original Jugcraft painters are `tools/zeppelin.py`, `tools/halloween_textures.py` and `tools/plant_office2.py`.

The shared owner catalog was reviewed and searched for outfit, hat, cloth, canvas and backpack references, including inspection of the supplied farming canvas assets. The existing companion has bespoke miniature proportions, so these outfits extend its code-native geometry and reuse suitable existing runtime materials. No owner-library files were modified or imported. Farmer uses a straw brim, bib overalls, buttons and seed pouch; Fisherman a bucket-style hat, pocketed vest and creel; Chef an apron, neckerchief and oversized toque; Mechanic coveralls, cap, belt and wrench; Porter a cap, vest and backpack. Jughead's four brimmed hats have three shallow crown levels, rising 0.68 model units above the brim. Their tops stay below the shoulder of the jug, surrounding only its thin neck through a continuous centre opening. The shoulder and reservoir remain fully exposed. The Chef toque remains on top of the jug, seated directly on its base. Its owner-requested square revision uses a narrow rectangular band, straight sides and a broad crown capped by a flat inset tier. Four adjoining cuboids replace the rounded radial mesh; the worn and inventory shapes share those same proportions, and placement on both companions stays unchanged. Chef and Mechanic jackets extend above the original body's top plane to remove coincident surfaces; the Chef apron and pocket also have separated front surfaces. Feet, face and jug remain exposed. Uniforms follow existing work/sleep poses, and faster cutting/hoe cycles use corresponding client timing. These visual changes do not change outfit buffs, recipes or saved equipment.

## Remaining seven uniforms — 24 October 2026

Implemented by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), continuing the owner's accepted outfit plan. Baker, Ranch Hand, Beekeeper, Runner, Alchemist, Orchard Keeper and Groundskeeper append to the synchronized enum, retaining all existing IDs and the single saved costume slot. No new world scans, tasks, inventories, network messages, dependencies or native machine-time multipliers are introduced.

- **Baker:** oven load/take reaches change from 20 to 17 ticks (1.2x, rounded up), synchronized with the client pie slide. Hearth delivery movement and existing handling debits save 20% reserve. Bake time and output counts are untouched.
- **Ranch Hand:** shearing/milking change from 60 to 50 ticks with matching client timing. Those actions, egg collecting and opted-in husbandry save 15% work reserve. Real shears, buckets, animal holding, loot/cargo limits, population rules and all native/per-cow cooldowns are retained.
- **Beekeeper:** the existing 60-tick harvest saves 20% reserve. Readiness still checks smoke immediately before completion; the existing honey-reset path never releases/angers bees. That protection already applies to smoked companion harvesting and is preserved, rather than inventing broad bee immunity or allowing unsmoked harvests.
- **Runner:** wheel and crank use a 15% reserve discount through the existing transactional energy calculation. They return the same accepted output units, retain output caps, stop for full destinations and suppress passive regeneration while productive. Both crank preflight and commit use the discount, so a rejected transfer cannot consume reserve or advance the fractional remainder.
- **Alchemist:** the native brewing stand and Jugcraft `crucible` are logistics-only jobs. Their loaded deliveries gain 20% movement speed and save 20% reserve; no pretend timed interaction is added. Formula execution, brewing chains, heat, water, fuel and recipe timing remain native.
- **Orchard Keeper:** orchard collection and tree-tap work run at 1.2x speed (80 to 67 and 40 to 34 ticks). Collection, existing cider-press effort and orchard/tap/cider delivery costs save 15% reserve. Loaded corresponding deliveries move 20% faster. Press intervals, fermentation and all yields stay unchanged.
- **Groundskeeper:** water fills run at 1.2x speed (40 to 34 ticks) with matching scoop timing. Water collection, farmland repair and fertilizing save 20% reserve; water/sprinkler deliveries save 20% reserve and move 20% faster with cargo. Repair/fertilizer action times, dose caps, cooldowns, work priorities and native sprinkler operation are unchanged. Ordinary crop hauling does not inherit the maintenance bonus.

Delivery bonuses classify only the already selected target, without block reads. One existing transient movement modifier is updated when its amount changes (Porter 25%, the three matching delivery outfits 20%, otherwise absent). It requires real carried cargo and is removed for editing, meals, rest, empty trips, a different costume or an unrelated route. General porter routes cannot borrow a destination's specialist bonus. Supply/Output transfers remain transactional and instantaneous; the existing handoff admission window is untouched.

All new looks extend the same cached geometry and approved runtime materials as the original five: canvas aprons/vests, smooth dyed cloth, leather and iron details. Baker adds a low stepped cap and mitts; Ranch Hand a broad hat, red bandanna and vest; Beekeeper a cream tunic, gloves and a sparse cuboid veil that leaves the eyes readable; Runner a headband and numbered running kit; Alchemist a purple robe, hat-mounted goggles and bottle belt; Orchard Keeper a green cap, apron and fruit basket; Groundskeeper a yellow rain hat, green coat and boots. Jughead's hats retain the centre opening and shallow tiers confined to the jug neck. The headband has an open centre on both variants. No wool textures are used. Original five worn meshes, including both Chef versions, are preserved byte-for-byte in the offline geometry export. New inventory models are cuboid miniatures derived from these same shapes/materials. Seven recipes, unlocks, names and descriptive bonus tooltips use the existing registration paths; no manual list of costume IDs is required.
