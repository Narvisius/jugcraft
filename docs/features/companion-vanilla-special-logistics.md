# Companion vanilla stations, solid-fuel generators and special logistics

Owner-confirmed scope, implemented 11 October 2026 with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) on `peepo-companion`. Extends existing Planner assignments, station-owned nine-item Filters, Supply/Output modes, real eight-slot cargo, transactional transfers, shared search/path budgets, protected-area and container-lock checks. No new assets, recipes, dependencies, currencies, fake players or chunk loaders.

## Progression and workflow

Existing vanilla furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces, brewing stands, campfires/soul campfires, composters and Crafters now expose companion logistics. Food/ore inputs, farmer compost and brewing materials flow from existing Supply chests to ordinary recipes, with cooked goods, bone meal, potion bottles and applicable remainders delivered to Output. Vanilla speed, fuel data components, crafting recipes, compost yields and redstone triggers remain authoritative. No new unlock replaces crafting/placing/powering the original station. Delivery effort uses the existing load-based companion reserve cost; vanilla machines and generators do not gain resident speed assistance or a passive work charge.

Furnace inputs target eight items, fuel one. Campfires use their four real cooking slots. Brewing targets three matching bottles, one reagent and fuel as required. The initial one-step integration is extended by [hive harvesting and brewing chains](companion-hives-brewing.md): selected final potions now support saved multi-step batches. Only brewing ghosts preserve the potion-contents component so different potion types remain distinct; other station ghosts retain their prior item-ID semantics. Furnace experience follows vanilla automated extraction behavior.

The vanilla Crafter remembers a manually seeded complete grid, preserving exact item components and disabled slots, and replenishes up to two items per required slot. It validates the entire saved pattern and the current recipe before a managed redstone craft, preventing accidental partial-grid recipes. Filter edits preserve the pattern; changing the actual grid to a new complete recipe relearns it. Normal redstone pulses remain required. Campfires supplied by companions and Crafters with adopted patterns capture their native products in twelve saved slots. The Crafter preflights all recipe remainders and its result before consuming inputs. Full campfire output pauses companion loading; overflow from independently/manual-loaded fires follows vanilla dropping rather than losing items. Empty-hand sneak-use retrieves stored items; anything that does not fit remains stored. Breaking a station drops buffered products. Once managed, capture persists even if a helper is removed; manual recovery remains available. This is companion collection storage, not a new hopper-facing inventory.

Coal Generator, Steam Generator and Large Steam Engine receive accepted solid fuels (up to four stocked items). Boilers accept one water bucket when tank/bucket-output space allows, and return empty buckets. JE generators stop requesting supplies at full energy; native kinetic demand governs the steam engine. No liquid/gas/renewable generators were added, and production rates are unchanged. Input/output direction detection now recognizes the Coal Generator's fuel-only slot correctly. Auto mode keeps Jugcraft's existing external-automation checks; vanilla stations use Auto as allowed transport without new external-automation detection.

The Auto-Crafter retains a real player-seeded pattern and tops each occupied slot up to two. Its existing last ingredient remains the template; output and remainder slots are collected through configured output sides. It retains the existing limitation against several different container-remainder types in one recipe. The Electroplating Bath chooses one compatible damageable item plus nickel/silver/gold ingot, preserves the item's components, rejects incompatible/redundant fully repaired plating, and follows its normal sulfuric-acid and power requirements. A dedicated Supply controls the metal. Both procedural machines respect their output filter during processing.

Fluid recipe catalogs now include item-free recipes and recipes with no item outputs. This enables catalyst restocking for fluid-only outputs and exposes item/byproduct slots across fluid processors. Ingredients keep their positional slots and actual recipe counts; fluid compatibility is checked before choosing a supply plan. Fluid-only outputs have no ghost item icon and require an empty filter. All fluids still use pipes/tanks; existing recipe consumption, assistance effort and progression remain unchanged.

## Server behavior and persistence

Vanilla block entities allocate companion state lazily; small mixins attach save/load access and intercept only native campfire/Crafter output. Recipes are cached server-wide by type and ID, cleared with the existing reload/start/stop recipe-cache lifecycle. Vanilla discovery checks at most 256 catalog entries per planning pass, continuing at a saved-in-memory cursor, and prefers an existing compatible plan. Supply snapshots retain the existing bounded views; there is no new world ticker or worker search. Campfire mutations, saved output extraction and composter changes use transaction snapshots. Composter simulations use a local deterministic random stream and defer block changes/scheduling until commit; they never advance the world's random stream or schedule ticks while probing.

Vanilla recipe selection, output buffers, Crafter pattern and managed state are persisted; absent data leaves ordinary vanilla behavior. Locked containers reject transfers. Physical transport still revalidates assignment, loaded chunk and permissions; disconnected/stale supplies remain real cargo and return through existing handling. The bath's short-lived candidate pair is replanned from real inputs/supplies after reload. Machine catalogs and filters remain saved through their existing path.
