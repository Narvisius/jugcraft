# The Crypt and the Ossuary

Status: implemented. CI builds it and its game tests and client screenshots pass (below); not yet played by hand.
Proposal issue: none. The owner asked for it directly on 4 October 2026 ("Lets come up with another 20 very detailed well thought out additions to add for the halloween / fall season using the first 3 images for ideas of prop sets … lets do them piece by piece maybe 5 at a time"). These are prop sets 6–10 of the [Witching Season plan](witching-season.md), drawn from the reference pictures' iron-strapped coffin, stone sarcophagus, bones, giant skeleton hand and winged gargoyles.
Owner: @jimbozoomer-byte
Target milestone and tier: Discovery tier. Every recipe takes early materials (bones, bone blocks, stone, iron, copper, glass, planks, wool, a chest, an armour stand). The Gargoyle Sentinel's ender eye needs a trip to the Nether for blaze powder first.
Primary specialty and supported player role: building, with useful pieces for storage, redstone and farming. These props furnish a crypt, an ossuary parlour, a giant's grave and a church roof; the Hollow Acre lair (boss 1) is furnished with them.

## Player experience

### 6. The Iron-Bound Coffin, the Skeleton Key and the Coffin Wardrobe
1. **Iron-Bound Coffin** (a Coffin ringed by four iron ingots): a coffin two blocks long, of black wood bound with riveted iron bands and corners, a hasp and a padlock, lined in purple velvet.
   - It holds **54 slots**, a double chest's, and opens from either half; the lid lifts on both.
   - Like the Coffin, sneak-use it with an empty hand to lie down and set your spawn.
2. **Key Blank** (an iron nugget over an iron ingot; two at a time) and **Skeleton Key**: an ornate key with a skull in its bow.
   - **Cut a key**: sneak-use an unlocked Iron-Bound Coffin with a blank. The blank is cut to new wards (one of 15,624 patterns), becomes that lock's Skeleton Key, and locks the coffin. The padlock shows on the hasp.
   - **Lock and unlock**: sneak-use a coffin with a cut key to lock an unlocked coffin to it, or to unlock the coffin it fits. Another key, or a blank, can't unlock a locked coffin.
   - The key's tooltip shows its wards, such as 3-1-4-2-5-1.
3. **Locked**:
   - only someone holding its key in either hand opens it; anyone else hears it rattle and is told it is locked;
   - hoppers and pipes can neither take from it nor fill it;
   - as with vanilla's locked containers, a lock stops opening, not breaking. Land claims protect against breaking.
4. **Copy a key**: a cut key and one to eight blanks in a crafting grid make that many copies, and the key stays in the grid. Give your friends a copy to share a coffin.
5. **Coffin Wardrobe** (a Coffin, a glass pane and an armour stand): an upright coffin two blocks tall with a glazed window in its lid, and inside, on an iron stand, a skeleton mannequin.
   - **Hang armour**: use a piece of armour on it to hang it on the mannequin; a piece already hanging there comes back to you.
   - **Swap**: use it with an empty hand and every piece you wear swaps with the piece it holds, all at once. A piece with Curse of Binding stays on you.
   - The mannequin wears what it holds, drawn as an armour stand would wear it.
   - Broken, it drops what it held.

### 7. The Stone Sarcophagus
1. **Stone Brick, Deepslate and Blackstone Sarcophagi** (that stone, two bones and a chest): tomb-chests two blocks long, with carved panels, a quatrefoil on each end, a skull boss and a cornice, under a heavy lid with a raised border. Inside lies a skeleton in the rags of its shroud.
2. **Use it** and the lid grinds aside and tips down on that side, showing the skeleton; it holds **27 slots**. A comparator reads it.
3. **Effigies**: the Stonemason's Chisel recarves the lid, on both halves, in turn as a plain lid, a knight with his sword on his breast, a lady with folded hands and a lily, and a skull and crossed bones. Each carving wears the chisel.
4. **It knocks**: at night, shut, with a player within six blocks, there is a 1 in 30 chance each second (at most once a minute) of three slow knocks from inside, half a second apart, and the lid shudders with each.

### 8. The Bone Throne and the Ossuary Parlour
1. **Bone Throne** (two skeleton skulls, five bones, red wool and a bone block; two blocks tall): a seat framed in thigh bones, armrests ending in skulls, a red velvet cushion, and a fan of ribs behind, topped by a horned skull.
   - **Sit in it** (not while sneaking); one sitter at a time.
   - While someone sits at night, the crest's eye sockets glow red (light 4). It goes dark when they rise.
2. **Ribcage Bookcase** (six bones and two planks): a ribcage holding books between its ribs.
   - It is a chiseled bookshelf: aim at one of its six places and use a book, a written book or an enchanted book to shelve it there, and use it again to take it back. Hoppers fill and empty it, and a comparator reads the last place used.
   - It **counts as a bookshelf for an enchanting table**.
3. **Skull Footstool** (red wool, a skeleton skull and a bone): a skull on crossed bones under a velvet cushion; a low seat.
4. **Vertebra Floor Lamp** (glowstone, a bone block and a bone; two blocks tall): a column of vertebrae under a skull shade lit from inside (light 13), its eyes amber. Use it to switch it; a redstone signal lights it while it lasts.

### 9. The Buried Colossus
1. **Colossal Skull** (nine bone blocks; 2 × 2 × 2): a giant's cracked skull, placed facing you from the block you aim at to your right and up and away. Every block must be free.
   - **Give any of its blocks a redstone signal and its jaw drops open**, grinding, and wind moans through it; it shuts when the signal goes.
   - **Use it and its jaw snaps.**
   - At night a faint blue glow breathes deep in each eye socket.
   - Breaking any block breaks the whole skull, which drops once.
2. **Colossal Rib** (three bone blocks; two at a time): a rib two blocks tall, rising from the back of its block and curving forward over the block in front. **Set two facing each other with one block between them and they meet as an arch** you can walk under. Breaking either parts the other, which ends short and broken again.
3. **Colossal Vertebra** (three bone blocks and four bones; three at a time): a vertebra with its round body, its arch and long spine and a wing to each side. It lies along the face it is set on, like a log, so a row of them is a spine.
4. **Colossal Femur** (three bone blocks): a thigh bone two blocks long lying in the grass, its ball and knob at one end and the two knuckles of the knee at the other.
5. With the existing **Giant Bone Hand** and **Bone Piles**, they make a giant's half-buried skeleton.

### 10. The gargoyles
1. **Gargoyle Sentinel** (four stone bricks, an ender eye and two redstone): a winged gargoyle crouched on a moulded pedestal, wings folded, claws over the edge.
   - **It watches for monsters**: twice a second it finds the nearest hostile mob within 16 blocks. Players and other creatures are ignored.
   - Its head turns to follow that mob (up to 80° either way), and its eyes glow brighter the nearer it comes.
   - **It gives a redstone signal** from 15 (two blocks or nearer) falling to 1 at sixteen blocks, and none with no monster in range.
2. **Gargoyle Rainspout** (three stone bricks and a copper ingot): a grotesque head on a long gutter-spout, hung on the side of a block, which it falls without.
   - **When it rains on the roof over it, it pours a stream** from its mouth.
   - A cauldron up to four blocks under its mouth (the first thing the stream lands on) fills one level every ten seconds: vanilla's cauldron to three levels of water, an empty Bubbling Cauldron with water, and the Horned Skull Cauldron with water a level at a time.
3. **Chimera Finial** (a bone over stone over a stone slab): a winged chimera on a ball finial for a roof ridge or a buttress.
   - **It is a weather sensor**: a redstone signal of 7 while rain or snow falls on it, 15 in a thunderstorm, and none under a roof or in a dry biome.
   - In a storm it spreads its wings.

## Changes from the plan
- **Keys** are cut from **Key Blanks** (two from an iron ingot and an iron nugget): a blank becomes a key by cutting it to a lock, so no key exists without a lock to fit. Copying takes one to eight blanks at once.
- **The wardrobe** also takes armour hung on it directly, so it can be dressed without wearing the armour first.
- **The Colossal Skull's glow** is drawn by the client at night only, as a faint breathing glow; it gives no light.
- **Art fixes, 5 and 6 October 2026**: skulls have square sockets with flat glows (the lit Vertebra Floor Lamp's sockets glow whole; the Colossal Skull's night glow now lies 0.1 pixel in front of the hollow behind its sockets, where it shows), and faces are closed on the Colossal Vertebra, Skull and Rib, the Coffin Wardrobe, the Iron-Bound Coffin (whose bands and corner caps no longer lie flush with its velvet when open) and the Ribcage Bookcase. The full list is in [witchs-workshop.md](witchs-workshop.md#changes-from-the-plan).

## Connections
- Existing input producer:
  - bones and bone blocks (skeletons, the Bone Piles), skeleton skulls;
  - stone, deepslate and blackstone; iron, copper and glowstone; an ender eye;
  - the Coffin (batch 3), the Stonemason's Chisel (the graveyard).
- Existing output consumer:
  - storage: the Iron-Bound Coffin (54 slots, lockable) and the sarcophagi (27 slots);
  - enchanting (the Ribcage Bookcase powers a table);
  - redstone (the sentinel's monster signal, the finial's weather signal, the skull's jaw, the lamp);
  - farming and brewing (the rainspout fills cauldrons: vanilla's, the Bubbling Cauldron, the Horned Skull Cauldron);
  - building; the Hollow Acre lair (boss 1) is furnished with them.
- Technology connection: redstone, hoppers and pipes (refused while locked), cauldrons. Magic connection: enchanting, the brewing cauldrons.
- Reachable entry path: all need only early materials and a crafting table; the sentinel needs an ender eye. None needs these props first.
- Required vs optional: everything is optional; nothing gates progression.

## Balance and automation
- **Storage**: the Iron-Bound Coffin holds a double chest's 54 slots for a Coffin (27 slots) and four iron ingots, about a double chest's cost.
- **Keys**: copies cost a blank each (half an ingot and half a nugget); a lock gives no extra storage.
- **The wardrobe** moves armour, never makes it.
- **The rainspout** gives water, as rain already does into a cauldron, faster: a level every ten seconds while it rains, from one spout into one cauldron. Water is not a gain the economy prices.
- **The sentinel** looks twice a second for mobs within 16 blocks; the finial checks the weather once a second; the sarcophagus checks for a player once a second; the rainspout checks the weather once a second. All are bounded and local.
- **Recipes**: each takes materials in and gives the prop; breaking gives the prop back. The ribs (two from three bone blocks) and vertebrae (three from three bone blocks and four bones) are worth no more than they cost, and nothing turns them back into bone blocks. No loops.

## Multiplayer and persistence
- **Server-side**: locking, cutting and copying keys, opening, swapping armour, recarving, knocking, sitting, switching, the jaw, the sentinel's look, pouring and the finial's weather are all decided on the server.
- **Build rights**: locking and unlocking, hanging and swapping armour need the right to use blocks there; recarving needs the right to build.
- **Saved**:
  - block state: the coffin's padlock, the lids' carvings and whether they are open, the throne's glow, the lamp, the skull's jaw, the ribs' joining, the sentinel's signal, the spout's pouring and the finial's weather;
  - block entities: the coffin's 54 stacks and lock, the sarcophagus's 27 stacks and its last knock, the wardrobe's four pieces, the bookcase's books, and the spout's pour timer;
  - the lid's slide, the jaw's swing, the sentinel's head and the mannequin are client animations, not saved.
- **Drops on breaking**: the coffin, sarcophagi, wardrobe and bookcase spill what they hold. A lock does not stop breaking.
- **New IDs**:
  - blocks and items `iron_bound_coffin`, `coffin_wardrobe`, `stone_brick_sarcophagus`, `deepslate_sarcophagus`, `blackstone_sarcophagus`, `bone_throne`, `ribcage_bookcase`, `skull_footstool`, `vertebra_floor_lamp`, `colossal_skull`, `colossal_rib`, `colossal_vertebra`, `colossal_femur`, `gargoyle_sentinel`, `gargoyle_rainspout`, `chimera_finial`;
  - items `key_blank` and `skeleton_key`;
  - the data component `jugcraft:key_wards`;
  - the recipe serializer `jugcraft:key_copying`;
  - block entity types `iron_bound_coffin`, `coffin_wardrobe`, `sarcophagus`, `colossal_skull`, `gargoyle_sentinel`, `gargoyle_rainspout` (the Ribcage Bookcase uses vanilla's chiseled bookshelf's);
  - their recipes.
- **Feature switch**: the recipes follow the agriculture feature switch.

## Dependencies and assets
- **No new dependencies.**
- **Models**: built on `tools/flora_art.py` by `tools/decor18_data.py`, which paints their textures by code (128 × 128 for every sculpted prop).
- **Art style**: the creature props (faces, bones, fur, scales, fire) are painted with the clean, cute painters in `tools/cute_art.py` since 5 October 2026, after the owner asked for simpler, smoother creatures ([ART_DIRECTION.md](../ART_DIRECTION.md#creatures-and-faces-cute-and-clean)).
- **Moving parts**: the sarcophagi's lids, the skull's jaw and the sentinel's head and eyes are in `assets/jugcraft/decor18_quads.json`. The rainspout's stream and the skull's socket glow use two small greyscale textures the client tints.
- **The wardrobe's armour** is vanilla's own armour drawn on an armour stand the client keeps (never added to the world).
- **Originality**: all original. The look follows the owner's reference pictures, and nothing is traced from them.

## World and event applicability
Placed by players only; no worldgen. They work all year, and nothing depends on the Halloween event.
