Primary specialty and supported player role: guidance for every player

## Player experience
- A **Jugcraft** tab in the advancements screen (key L) with 22 steps.
- Each step is earned by having its item, so it works however the player got it: crafted, traded or found.
- Branches:
  - materials: tin → bronze → prospector;
  - power and machines: coal generator → crusher → pulverizer, plus battery box and auto-crafter;
  - kinetics and logistics: hand crank → steam engines, belt pulleys and conveyors;
  - steel: steel → steel foundry, capacitor bank and charging station → mining drill (and upgrade modules), chainsaw and rocket pack.
- Milestones (steel, the steel foundry, the large steam engine) are goals, and the rocket pack is a challenge. Goals and challenges are announced in chat.
- The Engineer's Handbook's Getting Started chapter lists every step in order on its Milestones pages.
