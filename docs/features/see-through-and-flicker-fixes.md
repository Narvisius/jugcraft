# See-through and flickering models: the shared render fixes

Status: implemented in the generators and client code; checked offline only (no Gradle build, client run or in-game screenshot here).
Proposal issue: none; the owner's report of 5 October 2026 ("no backsides", "flashing because textures are colliding", "go look through everything for more of that").
Owner: art fixes (shared render core).
Target milestone and tier: all tiers (cosmetic).
Primary specialty and supported player role: everyone who builds or looks at machines, guns and vehicles.

## Player experience
- **No more see-through guns and vehicles.** The grand mortar, every tower gun and big gun (siege mortar, howitzer, flak gun), the landship, the Diesel Walker, the zeppelin, the observation balloon and the searchlight are drawn closed from every side. Before, whole 16-pixel patches of their faces were left out wherever a smaller part (a band, ring, step of a round barrel, deck or rail) sat on part of them, and the game showed the world through the gap.
- **No more flashing where parts meet.** Two differently drawn faces never share a plane any more, so collars, muzzle rings, hazard rims, brass caps, gear teeth, handwheels and rotor shafts stop flickering, near and far, standing or moving. That covers block models (with their rotated gears and handwheels, and the joins between a multi-block machine's parts), the quads of the big models, the kinetic rotors, the worn rocket pack and exosuits, and every quad-drawn decoration.
- **Two-sided sheets stop fighting themselves:** string-light wires and pennants, floating-candle and Aura Candle flames, the bonfire and witch-fire flames, grapple and zipline lines and the drone depot's hangar doors.
- **No strips of other textures** on faces that reached past their texture (trebuchets, the blueprint table, dead trees, the console desk, weathervanes, Celtic crosses and others). Such a face now shows a window of its own texture at its own density (the drone depot terminal's and console desk's screens show their screens again rather than smeared stripes).
- **The Witching Season props are closed** (rebased onto #183): the Coffin Wardrobe, Curiosity Cabinet, oddity jars, Iron-Bound Coffin, sarcophagi, candelabra and the rest no longer show holes where a box left a face out; the Horned Skull Cauldron's hollow is lined walls, and the Colossal Skull keeps its open sockets. The Farm Stand and Harvest Effigy parts and the tall flowers' halves no longer fight across their blocks; the effigy's blaze, the garland leaves and the blueprint placement ghost are drawn as two lifted sides. The floating candles' flames read the same way round from behind again.
- **No slits where a spinning part meets its block:** the hand crank's axle, the solar tracker's pivot and the heliostat's shaft are set into the still part instead of 0.1 px off it.

## Connections
Infrastructure and looks only: no items, recipes, energy or progression change, so resource links do not apply. It serves every branch whose models are generated by `tools/` (machines, kinetics, guns, vehicles, decorations).

## How it works
- **Exact hidden-face removal for big models** (`tools/zeppelin.py` `tiled_quads`, used by the artillery, tower guns, landship, walker, trench works and zeppelin): each face of an unrotated box loses exactly the area another box covers just outside it; where two boxes have faces on one plane facing the same way, only the smaller (the detail; the lower index on a tie) is drawn there. Faces are cut at their own edges, every cover's edges and the 16-pixel grid, and merged back into rectangles per 16-pixel cell, so the textures map exactly as before. Rotated elements and stretched (`!`) faces still go through `kinetic_rotors.quads`, after the boxes involved are pushed apart.
- **Separation everywhere else** (`tools/model_writer.py`): `separate_coplanar` now covers rotated elements (elements turned by the same transform are compared in one shared space; faces left square to the world by their turn, such as gear and handwheel caps, as turned polygons), pushes nearly flush faces (under 0.09 px apart) too, uses a 0.1 px push (was 0.02), keeps a pushed element's UVs, and works on private copies (generators share face dicts). Multi-block machines are separated before they are sliced into part models and again across all their part models after slicing. `fit_uvs` keeps every UV inside its sprite. `separate_boxes` brings the same to model tuples for `kinetic_rotors.quads` (rotors, worn models), and `decor6_data.quads` separates its block-model elements before exporting.
- **Two-sided planes:** the quad exporters lift each side of a zero-thickness plane 0.05 px along its own normal; Java renderers draw such sheets with `DecorDraw.twoSided` (front lifted, reversed back lifted the other way) instead of an exact reversed twin. A flame's core is lifted further than its flame.
- **Small model fixes:** `gear()` and `wheel()` make their 45-degree copies 0.1 px shorter at each end. A rotor never shares a plane with the still block: shafts end `ROTOR_GAP` (0.1 px) inside a housing (electric and magnet motors) or are that much slimmer than the bearings they run through (flywheel), and where a shaft meets a still part it is set into it (the hand crank's axle starts inside its hub plate, the solar tracker's pivot is slimmer than its mount bar and sunk into it, the heliostat's shaft reaches the mirror), never held 0.1 px off it, which opened see-through slits.
- **Closing** (`model_writer.finish_closed`, `close_open_faces`; used by the Witching Season generators `decor17_data` to `decor20_data` through `flora_art.closing_writer`): every left-out face of an unrotated opaque box that nothing covers is drawn, with the opposite face's look or a window of a neighbouring face at its texel density, before and after the separation pass. A face left out on purpose (the Colossal Skull's sockets) is named in the element's `"_keep_open"`, which never reaches the model file, and the model is allow-listed in O1. A hollow is built from walls (the cauldron), never from a solid box with its top left out.
- **Models drawn together:** multi-block machines are cut by `model_writer.split_model` (separated before the cut, then across the parts with the pieces of one cut element moving together, so no step opens at a seam); the tall flowers (`flora_models.tall_models`) and the Farm Stand and Harvest Effigy (`decor20_data.separated`) are separated whole before they are shared out among their blocks.
- **UVs:** `fit_uv` keeps a face's texel density: a span that straddles a tile edge is shifted by whole tiles and slid the least distance inside the sprite; only a span longer than 16 reads the whole sprite.
- **Backwards elements** (from > to) are refused by the separation pass with the element named; the Branching Chandelier's four arm-tip drops were written that way and are fixed at the source.
- **The guard:** `tools/art_check.py`, run by `tools/check_mod_data.py`, checks every block, item and classic-pack model (rules Z1, N1, U1, O1, I1), every quad part (Z2, N2, H1, A1, A2) and every set of models drawn together (X1 shared or near planes between them, X2 bared seams): each multi-block's parts at their footprint offsets in both styles, the `_lower`/`_upper` and `_bottom`/`_top` halves, the Farm Stand and Harvest Effigy parts, and each kinetic rotor with its block's `_active` model (a pair buried inside a closed shell, like the heliostat's shaft top under its mirror, does not count). Allow-lists are explicit, each with its reason. The rules are written down in [ART_DIRECTION.md](../ART_DIRECTION.md#rules-for-everything).

## Rollout and open questions
- Big models carry more quads (tiled exports 10.6k → 18.3k in all; the zeppelin body 2153 → 3821), still modest for one entity each.
- Where two faces used to fight, the smaller one now always shows (by design).
- Left to the per-object work: the cross-part joins of moving parts (barrels against turntables and bodies, the walker's legs against its pelvis, the searchlight's drum against its yoke), the ferris wheel's sixteen rotated copies of one section, the observation balloon's redesign, the barrel textures, and the decorations allow-listed under O1 and H1 in `tools/art_check.py`.
- The separation pushes outward, so a few decorations built from open boxes show a 0.1 px sliver more of their opening (angel at the tomb, faithful hound; both allow-listed under O1 with the rest of their families).
- The closing pass is used only by the Witching Season generators so far; the older decorations allow-listed under O1 (their seams too, under X2) keep their open boxes until their own fixes.
- The Colossal Skull is allow-listed under O1 for its sockets; a later rebuild could cut its front around them instead.
