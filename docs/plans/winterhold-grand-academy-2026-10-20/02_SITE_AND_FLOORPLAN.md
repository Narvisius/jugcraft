# Spatial master plan | recognizable College skeleton, grand expanded interior
All measurements are **proposed Minecraft-block dimensions**; adjust to source/worldgen physics without sacrificing landmarks. Define horizontal x/z origin at courtyard statue (0,0), north = negative z; level `Y0` is courtyard floor, NOT absolute world height. Final absolute elevations must come from site terrain survey. Coordinate system is a layout *proposal*, not pre-existing NBT positions.

## Site / macro footprint
- Outer College plan: roughly 132 x 138 blocks (excluding bridge). Courtyard approximately 37-block diameter, visually circular, outside ring 62–70 block radius; major hall tower and balconies visible from approach.
- Dramatic cliff base: academy courtyard planned approx 35–55 blocks above local water/approach terrain subject to generator constraints. Do not cut out entire surrounding terrain or leave dangling foundations.
- Signature bridge: 5–7 blocks walkable width, ~60–85 blocks approach span with two or three stable anchor piers. Bridge is the single dramatic public entrance; retain alternate emergency/player escape that does not break dungeon gating.
- Tone: ancient dark pale-stone masonry, slate towers, icy parapets, warm golden windows, blue runes, drifting snow and faint mist; **not** a uniformly cyan neon castle.

## Floor stratification
- L-3: Midden Dark, ancient archive/hidden Augur chamber, two independent return pathways.
- L-2: Midden, forge, abandoned research, relic vault, storerooms, service stair.
- L-1: undercroft logistics, tunnel, central stair to Midden, maintenance; concealed routes.
- L0: entrance/vestibule, courtyard, Elements great hall, dorm/faculty common areas, workshops and library reception.
- L1: amphitheater/lecture balconies, classrooms, tower quarters, senior stacks and archives.
- L2: tower dorms/faculty rooms, library restricted gallery, ritual alcoves and skywalks.
- L3: Arch-Mage living/study, observatory, beacon and roof promenade (where tower roof elevation permits).

## Circulation rules
- One clearly signposted public route: bridge > gate > courtyard > Elements hall. Circular courtyard should visually orient players to each destination.
- Accessible student route: courtyard > Attainment hall > dormitories > classrooms. All occupied floors joined via legible stairs; ladders only in optional secrets.
- Accessible faculty route: courtyard > Countenance > workshop/lab. Teachers should not be placed behind locked quest doors.
- Library route: courtyard > Arcanaeum; upper stacks reachable from within library; restricted annex permission via existing research or quest evidence.
- Midden: at least one discoverable entrance via courtyard and one secret/alternative exit. Dungeon must not softlock respawning players or stranded clients.
- Artifacts, furniture and halls must have safe headroom and NPC pathfinding clearance. Avoid snow layers blocking gates, stairways and mechanics.

## Expansion principle
Add **depth** before adding outside towers: lower laboratories, deeper library, upper classrooms, observation platforms, annexes and tucked-away halls preserve the silhouette. Expansion wings must read as original architectural accretions around the College ring.
