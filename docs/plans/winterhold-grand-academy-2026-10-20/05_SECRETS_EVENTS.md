## Random but deliberate academy events
- Passing lecture at class time; NPC meets players, gives an optional knowledge prompt.
- Weekly scholar debate with rotating lines, no permanent resource reward.
- A winter aurora night (localized particles/tint, not dimension-wide skybox override).
- A harmless ward flicker leading to a small investigation; bounded spawn and per-world cooldown.
- Librarian shelving incident revealing a clue to a secret; deterministic daily seed.

## Multiplayer and fairness
Puzzle controls are server-authoritative. Decide if each puzzle is world-shared or per-player; test simultaneous activation, reload, party credit and repeated reward attempts. Never lock the only exit behind an unsolvable puzzle. All high-value rewards must use existing Concordance research/cost mechanics; no mandatory boss chase to progress normal magic.
