# Function contract | every area has a reason to exist
**Use real implementation when APIs exist, not nonfunctional command-block scenery.** Features marked proposed must be implemented or shown as honest placeholders in early milestones.

## Public and academic rooms
- Bridge: slowly increasing localized snow, warding braziers, mage admission dialogue, sign that explains the region.
- Gate: research ledger, wayfinding map and teacher availability. Entering should never require an arbitrary hard-blocking fee.
- Courtyard: central orientation monument, daily ambience, notice board, respectful low-cost protective enchantment/ward demonstration.
- Elements Hall: teacher-led cast demonstrations, existing researched invocation interactions, safe target dummies, lesson progress.
- Attainment: assign a rest bed/room where existing ownership rules allow; accessible student notes; no forced player property system.
- Countenance: unique instructor offices with timed presence and course dialogue; instructor names persist after save/reload.
- Arcanaeum: Modonomicon/Concordance text access; lecterns surface existing facts; librarian points to unlock sources.
- Arch-Mage: evocative study, enchanting/alchemy *using actual* workstations, indoor garden, Observatory instrument; access tied to fair optional discoveries.

## Distinct classrooms (not six empty lecture halls)
- Radiance / revealing room: examine illuminated specimen, learn to find valid research sources.
- Ember / transformation lab: heat demonstration using existing safe Ember content; no invented free materials.
- Rime / stillness hall: examine cold preservation; implement only supported Rime mechanics, otherwise lore+working existing tools.
- Tempest / sky terrace: atmospheric instrument readings and lightning safety; separate from global weather control.
- Strata / circle chamber: examine structural binding or prepared stones; avoid bypassing survival construction progression.
- Verdance / healing conservatory: support/plant study; optional restoration exercises.
- Echo / archives: recover recordings of prior scholars; puzzle that requires observation, not brute force.
- Hollow / concealed lab: forbidden theories; no forced morally-aligned play choice or unobtainable exclusive power.

## Practical services
- Dining: cooking/food trade or simple room interaction; teachers gossip clues. Kitchen's sinks, cupboards and pantry have at least one useful supply function.
- Laundry: minor atmospheric interaction, e.g. activate steam and wet linen, optional cosmetics only.
- Workshop: legitimate alchemy, enchanting and existing Concordance craft stations. Avoid decorative fake blocks where usable equivalents exist.
- Midden: resource-bounded exploration with discoverable exits; forge designed as a ritual interaction via existing resource rules, not duplicating Skyrim code.

## Definition of usable
An area is usable if there is (a) a clear purpose, (b) an interactable or tangible discovery, (c) contextual feedback, (d) persistence/reset semantics when appropriate, and (e) evidence from gameplay/test or a truthful TODO status. Decoration-only rooms are exceptions only if explicitly labelled scenery in the room ledger.
