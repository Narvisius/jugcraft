# Owner-supplied building references

On 6 October 2026 the project owner supplied two screenshots, described them as buildings they had built, and requested that they replace the previous Styx observatory and greenhouse, with Styx living inside.

- [Observatory reference](styx-observatory-owner-reference.png): tall brick shaft, narrow stone foundation, dark cornices, copper bands and dome, open orange shutters, projecting telescope, side hall and white turret.
- [Greenhouse reference](styx-greenhouse-owner-reference.png): warm timber frame, white stepped roof bands, glass bays, large round gable window, shaped entry and planted exterior supports.

The supplied PNGs are retained unchanged as visual references, not in-game textures. Only screenshots were supplied; this implementation reconstructs visible architectural features with vanilla Minecraft blocks. It is not a schematic import or a claim of exact block-for-block reproduction. Hidden elevations, room layouts, staircases, the connecting garden and planting arrangements are original adaptations. The second screenshot's text and red arrow are annotations and are excluded from the structure.

Editable block construction source: `tools/styx_structure_v3.py`, exposed through `tools/styx_structure.py`. Actual implementation assistance: Codex. Compare the real-client screenshots in the feature document against these references for owner review.
