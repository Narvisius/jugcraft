## Fan homages
Jugcraft is not limited to wholly original ideas. On 6 October 2026 the owner allowed **fan homages**: creatures, characters, items and places inspired by films, TV shows, cartoons, books and games (for example the costumed villains of classic mystery cartoons), with their names changed ("we can change the names slightly").
- **The owner approves each homage.** Say in the pull request what it is a homage to.
- **Change the name.** Do not use the original's name, logo or title, and do not present it as official or endorsed.
- **The files are still Jugcraft's own.** Every texture, model, animation, sound and line of code is made for Jugcraft, or used with recorded permission as third-party material (above). Nothing is ripped, traced, sampled or recorded from the source: no screenshots or frames, no extracted game files, no clips of the show's sound or music. The source guides the idea and the look only.
- Homages are licensed like the rest of Jugcraft's own work, but the MIT License grants no rights in the original works they refer to.
