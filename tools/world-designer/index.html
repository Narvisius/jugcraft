    FROM MAP TO MINECRAFT

    Your world, drawn by you.

    1. In a local Jugcraft world with cheats enabled, run /jugcraft design export. Open the exported index.html, or load its catalog.json here.
    2. Sculpt terrain, paint biomes, and place spawn. A walled city uses Jugcraft’s existing working town. Structure markers use structures from your installed mods.
    3. Save your design JSON to keep editing. Export a world pack when the validation panel is clear.
    4. Create a new world in the same Jugcraft installation. Add and enable the ZIP in Data Packs, select Jugcraft Designed under World Type, and keep structures enabled.

    Sea level is 63. Rivers carve towards 59. The outer two grid cells blend into the seed’s normal terrain. The preview shows the planned surface, not trees, caves or the finished Minecraft world.

    Structure pins choose a start chunk. Their native biome and terrain rules still apply; random structures can still appear nearby. The city is one fixed Jugcraft town, and its builder levels its footprint. Deep caves below height 8 remain procedural, transitioning to the sculpted land by height 32.

    Use this for new worlds. Changing the pack does not reshape chunks you have already explored. No server is required to design or test in single-player.
